# Onboarding v6 — THE CONVERSION EVOLUTION (direction + build law)

2026-08-02. Founder brief: evolve — never replace — the v5 onboarding
+ keep wall so a user finishes believing "this is a legitimate,
medically grounded, evidence-based program designed by
professionals," not "another cute calorie counter." Same onboarding,

## 1. What is LOCKED (the constitution this work inherits)

- The OV5 machine: typed steps, pure router, canonical-key store,
  cohort branches, the v4.5 data contract — untouched.
- The act structure (~46 beats, 5 acts) and the interaction
  language: cross-off selects, tick rulers, strike-the-fear,
  receipts, hold-to-seal. Evidence (Lose It! A/B, Noom, PrayerLock)

- Palette (paper + ink + rose, 8 tokens, bgPrimary-only), type stack
  (JeniHeroSerif / Fraunces punch / DMSans), motion tokens, JeniMark
  one-colour law, JKBorderBeam placement law, sticker scatter law.
- Voice base: lowercase, italic punch 1-3 words, no em-dashes
  between words, no "AI", no drug brand names, no hearts, anti-shame,
  verb law (add / mark / weigh in), no first-party numeric
  weight-loss claims, live prices only, no fake urgency, no
  fabricated proof. All of it stays. Research verdict: these
  constraints exclude only two high-usage tactics (countdowns, fake
  proof) — and both have honest substitutes with equal or better
  measured lift (Blinkist +23%, Runtastic +44% with REAL reviews).
- The keep wall's honesty mechanics (billed-today everywhere,
  renewal date + year, per-week subordinate, money-back, reclaim
  row, sign-in door). Cal AI was briefly removed from the App Store
  for the inverse conduct (and reinstated only after changing it);
  these mechanics are survival, not decoration.

## 2. Approaches weighed

A. **Copy-only polish.** Cheap, ships fast — but leaves the reveal
   selling a dead product ("5-min plank a day"), the peak screens
   empty, and the wall without its value arc. Rejected: doesn't cash
   the brief.
B. **Evolution in place (CHOSEN).** Every beat keeps its slot; the
   register moves warm-generic → warm-specific (unit + source +
   conditional); the three thinnest high-stakes surfaces (teaches,
   reveal, wall) get real composition; defect debt cleared.
C. **Collapse to a short quiz / web2app-style funnel.** Contradicts
   the measured evidence, the brief's own "structure is good" law,
   and the data contract. Rejected. (Web2app acquisition funnel =
   separate future program, F7.)

## 3. The five design laws of v6

1. **Warmth in voice, rigor in numbers — never traded.** Lowercase
   stays; credibility arrives through what sentences SAY: number +
   unit + basis, named sources that resolve, conditional mood for
   plan logic ("if", "only when your data supports it"). The Midi
   pattern: validation sentence first, institutional sentence second.
2. **Every number carries its provenance.** "2,041 kcal · from your
   height, weight, and the pace you chose." "~1.0 lb a week · the
   0.5-1% band clinicians use." Naked numbers are decoration;
   sourced numbers are medicine.
3. **The peak must be the peak.** Craft budget concentrates where
   intent peaks: loader → projection → first week → wall. Nothing in
   the quiz may out-dress the reveal.
4. **Sell the current product only.** Every promise names a shipping
   surface (checklist day, snap, trend read, method, letters, weekly
   review). The plank-era tiles die. Feature promises cash within 3
   sessions or don't render.
5. **The stated refusal is a feature.** MacroFactor doctrine in Jeni
   voice: what this program will never do to her (red numbers,
   punishments, streak hostages, sold data) is written down where
   she decides.

     punishments · a bad day is in the math · your data stays yours,

  5. *(dormant) real proof* — a rating/review band that renders ONLY
