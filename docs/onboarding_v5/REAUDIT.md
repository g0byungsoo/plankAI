# Onboarding v5 — Round-2 Luxury Audit (founder pass)

2026-07-02, after founder review of round 1: "too safe, too generic,
too busy, not premium enough." Verdicts below are from re-reading every
walker frame as a luxury consumer-brand designer. Fix list is ordered
by visual leverage.

## What reads cheap right now

1. **Welcome — FAILED.** Eleven cutouts + polaroid + pill + wordmark +
   stickers + girl = five systems fighting on one screen. No focal
   point, no silence, no drama. It reads "mood board," not "invitation."
   → Total redesign: ONE from-behind figure, ONE line-cascade hero at
   display scale, a hairline, one whispered sub, CTA. Nothing else.
   The confidence IS the luxury.
2. **Trailing SF-symbol icons on option rows** (moons on sleep, arrows
   on trend, leaf/bolt everywhere) — the single most "template fitness
   app" tell in the flow. → Removed everywhere. Lock glyph survives
   only on prefer-not-to-say rows (it carries meaning, not decoration).
3. **Preselected answers** (pace "steady", signature boxes pre-checked,
   commitment chips pre-picked, nudge time "afternoon") — reads as a
   rushed form and cheapens consent. → Nothing starts selected.
   Every CTA gates on an intentional choice. The nudge screen loses its
   time rows entirely (her promise already chose the hour; the banner
   wears it).
4. **Ruler readout pill** — drop shadow floats it off the paper like
   iOS chrome. → Flat bgElevated + 0.33pt hairline; the serif numeral
   carries the weight.
5. **Nudge permissions screen** — banner + promise row + three option
   cards + CTA = four systems. → Banner (with her promise + her time) +
   trial-safety line + CTA. One decision on screen: allow.
6. **Demo pick cards** — 128pt cards read as thumbnails; generated
   photos read as stock next to the founder's real plates. → Founder
   photography (poke bowl / avocado toast + eggs / fried oysters),
   cards at 150×195, deeper fan overlap, one shadow register.
7. **Cuisine cloud** — 17 chips is a tag manager. → 12 (couples with
   in-app food prefs for the long tail); chips get air (12pt gaps,
   15/11 padding).
8. **Row rhythm** — 13pt vertical padding is form density. → 16pt,
   0.33pt hairlines, no icons: the her75 list register.
9. **Copy spots**: "and the stress level?" is filler → "how heavy is
   life right now?". Gender sub trimmed to the one honest clause.
10. **Welcome CTA stack** (pill + free-to-start + sign-in = 3 layers)
    → pill + one combined whisper line.
