# app v7 — THE EDITORIAL CONSTITUTION (mission 3, 2026-07-27)

Founder: "I no longer want a beautiful health app. I want one of
the most beautiful iOS applications ever built… The screens still
feel like interfaces. I want them to feel like editorial
compositions." Synthesized from a third, fully fresh 8-persona
visual panel (ADA reel-cutter, Apartamento AD, Hermès/COS CD,
museum exhibition designer, Fantastic Man designer, architecture
photographer, Family-school interaction designer, Apple
Invites/Journal designer) + a principles lane on the founder's
named references. Artifacts: `panel_editorial/`. Builds ON
02_VISUAL.md; where they differ, this wins.

## 1. The composition laws (the panel's unanimous physics)

1. **One owner per page.** Every screen has exactly one element
   the eye meets first, set at 52-72pt. Two near-equal masses =
   an assembled screen. ("The day gets ONE headline and it is
   the vow, not the count.")
2. **Bracketed emptiness.** Whitespace is placed BETWEEN a
   monument (top third) and an anchor (the foot), never left to
   pool below a top-stack. Ledgers pin to the foot.
3. **Three tiers, no more** (museum wall-text law): the serif
   headline · one sentence of meaning · a provenance caption.
4. **Controls dissolve into content.** The sentence is the
   camera. The word is the button. The rule is the field. No
   discs, no capsules, no boxed inputs, no glyph chrome beyond
   one quiet mark per page.
5. **Chrome ceiling: the eyebrow.** A page opens with the 13pt
   tracked dateline alone. One brand mark per page — the seal —
   so the dateline surrenders its sparkle.
6. **Numbers are demoted to the ledger** (values 30-34pt serif,
   labels 13pt caps) unless the number IS the page's one owner.
7. **Imagery is the missing organ.** Her real plate photographs,
   full-bleed, un-beautified (Hermès: the crafted object carries
   the story) — the cover's art and the spreads' warmth.

## 2. THE VOW (Home — the iconic page)

- Eyebrow alone (no camera disc, no hamburger, no second
  sparkle). Dateline tap = the letter; dateline long-press =
  settings.
- **The vow:** the day's line at 56pt+ across up to two lines,
  FULL cocoa ink (an unkept vow is unsigned, never faded), the
  italic punch word, one 15-17pt reason beneath.
- **The rule + the seal:** a full-measure hairline under the vow;
  the 36pt hollow ✦ SEATED ON the rule at its right end — wax on
  the fold. Signing: hold → the rule inks left-to-right, the
  seal fills deep rose and stamps with the commit haptic, the
  date prints beneath in 13pt caps as the countersign.
- **The sentence is the camera:** tap the vow, the capture
  rises. Control becomes content.
- Supporting lines at 32pt in the same grammar; the second act's
  lines likewise.
- **The ledger foot:** calories · protein · overnight fast ·
  steps as beat-19 rows pinned above the tab bar. The 860
  monument dissolves into the first row. The void between vow
  and ledger is the composition.

## 3. THE CLOSING RECEIPT (evening)

One grammar top to bottom: "closing the day." at 52-56pt →
the ledger (protein / tomorrow / still-open lines as rows) →
"how did today feel?" in italic serif with **three bare serif
words** (proud · okay · tender, ~28pt; the chosen word inks
rose — capsules dead) → **a bare hairline** for her one line
(the boxed field dead; the rule grows a cursor on focus).

## 4. THE ISSUE, with art (becoming)

- **The cover earns cover art:** her best plate photograph of
  the week full-bleed through the top ~55%, cream rising from
  its foot; masthead small; the thesis on the lower cream at
  56pt+. Photo-less fallback: the type poster at optical center.
- **Spreads: one figure + one line.** Each interior page is a
  room — eyebrow, ONE enormous figure or numeral (96-120pt
  didone where the number is the work), the caption-as-headline
  beneath, nothing else.
- **The fore-edge moves to the trailing edge:** vertical 1pt ×
  12-16pt hairline ticks hugging the right screen edge (a held
  book's actual fore-edge), current leaf rose and longer. The
  foot rail is dead.
- **The page-turn:** fixed running-head register (masthead +
  eyebrow + fore-edge identical on every page) + content
  parallax (~0.9x) + a 4-6° leading-edge rotation and a faint
  gutter shadow on the turn — paper lifting, not tabs sliding.

## 5. TWO VOICES (chat)

Measures narrow to ~65-70% so the gutter reads as a white river;
jeni 20-22pt cocoa flush-left; her replies 17pt italic serif
deep-rose flush-right (marginalia, not a mirrored column); day
breaks as centered small-caps between short hairlines; the
composer becomes a bare baseline hairline with a ghost italic
placeholder and a rose ✦ send mark (capsule + grey disc dead).
BUG to fix first: the ghost "today" divider overlapping the
masthead + a duplicated demo exchange.

## 6. Deferred to the founder

The tab bar (native Liquid Glass labels vs the panel's one-voice
word folio) — HIG-native vs editorial purity is the founder's
call. The letter's oldstyle-numeral date. The 96pt post-log
calorie hero (Invites' state-flip grammar).

## 7. Build order

(cover photo, spread template, fore-edge to the edge, page-turn)
