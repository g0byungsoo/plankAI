# My own critique (Claude, having read pixels + code + full doc history)

## What already sings (don't break)
1. becoming's weight page: eyebrow → serif reading ("holding steady this week.") → chromeless trend → started/now/goal triplet. This IS the brief's grammar. The v7 Home should learn from THIS page, not invent a new language.
2. DailyBriefEngine: deterministic, provenance-only, synthesizes signals (v6.2). The "one understanding" the brief wants is 70% built — it's just rendered small (22-24pt line under a rail) instead of being the page's reason.
3. Signals engine + safety law. The passive layer is the moat and the care-platform seed. CoachSummary (one move, clinical priority ladder) = the care-plan brain's embryo.
4. The comeback line ("back after 10 days. this is day 12, not day zero.") — world-class retention copy.
5. Evening close bones (receipt + how-did-today-feel + tonight's plan chips) = implementation intentions done kindly.
6. WeeklyReview (re-signing): consented adaptation is rare in consumer health. Keep as the weekly spine.

## What contradicts the north star
1. HOME IS A TO-DO LIST WEARING A COACH'S LINE. Check rings (v6.4 founder override), sticker-tile row icons (candy/shoe/padlock/balloon-dog), "IF YOU FEEL LIKE IT" seam, kcal progress bar with "~600 left" budget math. Emotional grammar = productivity + calorie tracker. The founder's own brief now overrides the v6.4 checkable-list override.
2. TOO MANY OBJECTS COMPETing at the same altitude: day pill + archetype note + date + day rail + week caption + coach line + one-thing card + 4-6 rows + food band + signals band + cycle promo row. No single understanding leads. ~7 typographic registers visible in one scroll.
3. The one-thing card: cocoa slab + THE ONE THING kicker + sticker = loud box announcing priority, vs the priority simply being FIRST and unmissable.
4. Sticker tiles undermine premium: 5 different pastel tiles per screen violate "one gesture per surface" luxury math. (Sticker moments are founder-locked for the 3 earned moments — row tiles are NOT those moments.)
5. Duplication: trend renders in becoming + chat card + brief; plates render in Today band + becoming page + journal; the overnight window renders as row + sheet + becoming page. Signals of a system organized by FEATURE, not by HER QUESTION.
6. Chat register drift: bubbles + suggestion chips read generic-messenger; the letter register (dated letters, her file) was the distinctive move and it's diluted.
7. 12-page becoming pager: nobody swipes 12 times. Pages 5-11 are archive-grade, not daily-grade.
8. The "connect your cycle" promo row inside SIGNALS band = growth surface inside the care surface.
9. Camera FAB is a cocoa circle stuck on the masthead; snap is the #1 retention behavior (3.1x D1) but reads as chrome, not as care.

## The architectural insight
Care plan ≠ new engine from scratch. It's a composition change:
- PrescriptionEngineV2 (slot tables) → CarePlanEngine: same beat vocabulary, but selection/ordering/reasons driven by state (signals, trend, cohort, sleep last night, protein yesterday, gap-return, medication cohort) with the static tables as the fallback floor. Every item carries a spoken reason (provenance).
- DailyBriefEngine.line → the understanding: promote to the page's opening statement (serif reading register, becoming-page grammar).
- CoachSummary's priority-ladder pattern → daily: at most ONE proactive care intervention per day, chosen by fixed clinical priority, delivered in the understanding or as the lead care item.
- White-label seam: CarePlan/CareItem/Observation as typed records (deviceless of brand), so a clinic protocol could later inject items; brand tokens already centralized.

## Vocabulary danger to fix in v7 copy
"checked off" grammar, "IF YOU FEEL LIKE IT" caps seam, "~600 left" budget language. Keep: direct register (founder), "overnight fast" (founder override, rails hold).
