## What JeniFit is now

A daily weight-management PROGRAM for women, delivered as a coaching
relationship. Three surfaces, three tenses: **today** (what we do),
**jeni** (how we think about it), **becoming** (what it's adding up
to). Everything else is a tool one of those three reaches for.

## The daily loop (the product)

wake → jeni's brief names the day's ONE thread → 3-5 beats sized to
her tier and cohort → plates snap in (protein is the score, kcal is
context) → steps count themselves → evening close receipts the day
and pre-frames tomorrow. The loop's promise: *you never have to
decide what matters today; you only have to show up small.*

## The weekly loop (the retention engine)

Mon/Thu weigh-ins feed the trend line → Becoming tells the week as a
coach's story with a mechanism → weekly receipt (Sunday) → reset
weeks every 4th (permission, not slack) → the Method's acts advance.
The weekly loop exists because the daily loop alone dies at D7: the
week is where meaning accrues (paid D7 = 10% is the fire; the weekly
story is the pull).

## Core behavior-change mechanisms (each maps to shipped mechanics)

1. **Self-monitoring with dignity** (Burke 2011): snap + photo-first
   journal; protein-first display; ranges not false precision.
2. **Flexible restraint** (Westenhoefer): begin-again insights,
   tomorrow-resets, no streak threats, the comeback brief.
3. **Implementation intentions** (Gollwitzer): the day-1 promise,
   promise-hour anchor, beats as pre-decided actions.
4. **Urge surfing / arousal downshift** (CBT + Balban 2023): breath
   as the craving brake — v2.4's reframe (17_FEATURE_EVALUATION §1).
5. **Cognitive restructuring**: the Method's 84-slot arc + jeni's
   in-context reframes (plateau script, water-weight script).
6. **Identity evidence** (self-perception): wins receipts, shown-up
   counts, "the kept version of you."
7. **Lean-mass preservation** (STEP/SURMOUNT DXA data): protein floor
   as the visible daily hero for GLP-1; movement framed as keeping
   muscle, not burning calories.

## What each audience gets

- **TikTok WL (majority):** the loop + the trend story + the Method's
  diet-brain deconstruction. Emotional job: "this understands me" =
  never shamed, always given the next smallest action.
- **On GLP-1:** protein floor with her number, appetite-rhythm-aware
  snap copy, weekly weigh-in cadence, muscle-keeping movement frames,
  hydration/fiber texture. Job: "the part the shot doesn't handle."
- **Post-GLP-1:** maintenance mode (band-watch trend framing, Sunday
  trend check, P6 lesson affinity, routine-outlives-the-loss workout
  frames). Job: "keep the progress."

## Feature verdicts (full table in 17_FEATURE_EVALUATION)

**Essential + performing:** snap/result (signature), brief engine,
beats, trend story, protein arc, steps (the easiest lever), breath
MECHANIC (69%), chat, walls/migration.
**Essential + repositioned in v2.4:** breath (wellness module →
craving-reset system), workouts (add the 5-minute floor — the
completion fix is smaller sessions, not louder celebration), Method
(read → read-then-DO: lesson completion chains into one action).
**Kept, watched:** journal (now a memory, not a database; next: the
day-receipt line), mark-as-done, settings.
**Absorbed into jeni over time:** weight interpretation moments,
craving triage, "what should I eat" — chat is the connective tissue;
modules are her tools. Nothing NEW ships as a standalone module
unless it earns a beat.
**Removed already:** the old shell + 12 legacy files (v2.3).

## Hierarchy (the app in one sentence per layer)

jeni (the voice) > the plan (the spine) > the tools (snap, breath,
move, read, weigh, walk) > the story (becoming). A feature that
can't say "why today?" doesn't render today.
