# R4 — MOVEMENT + STEPS + SLEEP for weight management (2025-2026)

Research brief for app v25. Domain: should Today be able to issue a walking
action, what the step goal should be, whether workouts deserve to exist,
and how much surface sleep earns. Sources fetched 2026-08-10.

Labels: **PROVEN** (RCT/meta-analysis or overwhelming cohort evidence) ·
**PROMISING** (consistent early trials or strong observational) ·
**CLAIMED** (plausible, thin evidence) · **CONVENTION** (industry habit,
not evidence) · **GIMMICK** (engagement mechanic; no health case).

---

## 1. Steps: the dose-response evidence

### 1.1 Mortality + disease risk — PROVEN (epidemiology)
The 2025 Lancet Public Health dose-response meta-analysis (57 studies, 8
outcomes) is now the canonical reference. Versus 2,000 steps/day, 7,000
steps/day associates with: −47% all-cause mortality, −25% CVD incidence,
−47% CVD mortality, −37% cancer mortality, −14% type 2 diabetes, −38%
dementia, −22% depressive symptoms, −28% falls. Inflection points sit at
**5,000-7,000 steps** for mortality/CVD/dementia/falls; T2D and depression
stay roughly linear (more keeps helping, gently).
https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpub/article/PIIS2468-2667(25)00164-1/fulltext
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40713949/

- The 2022 Lancet 15-cohort meta: mortality benefit plateaus ~6,000-8,000
  steps for 60+, ~8,000-10,000 for under-60s.
  https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lanpub/article/PIIS2468-2667(21)00302-9/fulltext
- I-Min Lee 2019 (older women, JAMA IM): 4,400 vs 2,700 steps → −41%
  mortality; benefit levels off ~7,500. Intensity added nothing beyond
  volume. https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2734709

**Reading for Jeni's audience (women 22-35):** the steepest part of the
curve is 2,000→6,000. Every incremental 1,000 steps for a sedentary user
buys real risk reduction; past ~8,000 the marginal case is thin. A default
that scolds an 8k walker for missing 10k has no evidence behind it.

### 1.2 The 10,000 myth — PROVEN (as history)
10,000 = 1965 Yamasa marketing for the *manpo-kei* ("10,000-step meter"),
launched around the Tokyo Olympics; the kanji for 10,000 resembles a
walking figure. No study ever established it; the literature that came
later keeps landing **below** it. A 10,000 default is CONVENTION built on
a GIMMICK. https://www.popsci.com/story/health/10000-steps-evidence-study/
https://www.nuhs.edu/debunking-the-10000-step-myth-figuring-out-what-actually-matters/

### 1.3 Steps → weight, specifically — PROVEN but modest
- Pedometer-walking meta (9 RCT-ish cohorts): **−1.27 kg** pooled, median
  16 weeks (~0.05 kg/week). Longer programs → more loss.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18195317/
- In T2D: −0.65 kg. https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/dme.13104
- Energy compensation is real: exercise-induced expenditure is partially
  offset by intake + reduced NEAT; compensation grows with duration and
  intensity. Leisure walking compensates *less* than power walking
  (25% vs 41% of expended energy eaten back).
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s43856-026-01502-z
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physiology/articles/10.3389/fphys.2019.01048/full

**Implication:** never sell steps as a fat-burn lever. On a GLP-1, the
drug creates the deficit; walking's honest jobs are risk reduction, mood,
glucose, NEAT defense, and *maintenance* (below). This aligns with the
existing register law (no burn/earn language).

### 1.4 Steps and maintenance — PROVEN (observational, consistent)
National Weight Control Registry: 94% of successful maintainers increased
activity; walking is the #1 modality; high volumes (~1h/day equivalent)
typify maintainers. Activity matters *more* after the loss than during it.
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK572051/
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8196944/

### 1.5 Which representation drives behavior: steps vs minutes vs distance
- Steps are the target representation used in nearly every successful
  behavioral RCT (pedometer metas, adaptive-goal RCTs, HeartSteps,
  JustWalk). Active-minutes targets appear mostly as *outcome* measures,
  not as the lever. No head-to-head RCT shows minutes-goals outperform
  step-goals; steps are concrete, glanceable, phone-measurable, and
  divisible into "X left" arithmetic. **Steps as the behavioral unit:
  CONVENTION, but a justified one** — it is the unit the evidence base
  was built on.
- Intensity can be expressed *through* cadence when needed: ≥100
  steps/min ≈ moderate (CADENCE-Adults, Tudor-Locke). Useful for a
  "brisk 10 minutes" action without heart-rate hardware — heuristic, not
  precise. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12966-020-01045-z
- Structured (step-goal) beats "lifestyle" goals for maintaining gains
  post-intervention in an internet walking program.
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2212636/

## 2. Adaptive goals — the strongest behavioral finding in this domain

### 2.1 Adaptive beats static 10k — PROMISING (multiple concordant RCTs)
- **Adams 2017 (2×2 factorial, overweight adults):** adaptive goals =
  60th percentile of the participant's own rank-ordered steps over a
  moving 9-day window, recalculated daily. Adaptive arms ended
  **+1,130 steps/day** vs static-10k arms; adaptive users hit their goal
  **58.2% of days vs 22.5%** for static-10k users.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5372290/
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3857300/
- **CalFit / Zhou 2018 (n=64, 10 weeks):** ML-personalized weekly goals
  vs fixed 10k → net **+960 steps/day** (95% CI 90-1830, p=.03); both
  arms decayed, adaptive decayed 3.5× slower.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5806006/
- Personalized "baseline +1,000-3,000" goals were met ~47% of days vs 23%
  for 10k in comparable designs. The mechanism is textbook goal-setting
  theory: success rate must stay high enough to sustain self-efficacy.

**Design law that falls out of the RCTs:** goal = percentile of HER OWN
recent history (60th percentile of a ~9-10 day window is the tested
recipe), floor ~2,000-3,000, progression capped, recalculated daily or
weekly, aimed at a clinically meaningful ceiling (~7,000-8,000, per §1.1)
— NOT at 10,000.

### 2.2 Goal-ratchet vs goal-relief
The percentile method is self-relieving: a bad week lowers the window, so
the goal breathes DOWN as well as up — that is *why* attainment stays
~50-60%. A ratchet-only goal (never decreases) recreates the static-goal
failure mode. Direct RCT evidence on ratchet-vs-relief as isolated factor
is thin (CLAIMED either way), but every successful adaptive trial allowed
downward movement. Pacer ships 2 rest days/week inside streaks; Apple
added ring pausing + day-customized goals in watchOS 11 after a decade of
criticism. Forgiveness mechanics (Duolingo/Noom-style repair, two-day
rule) are CONVENTION with good behavioral rationale (what-the-hell
effect). https://support.mypacer.com/hc/en-us/articles/42065025286157
https://yukaichou.com/gamification-analysis/streak-design-gamification-motivation-burnout/

### 2.3 JITAI walking prompts — PROMISING, effects small and decaying
- **HeartSteps MRT (n=44, 6 wks):** a contextually-tailored walking
  suggestion → **+14% steps (~35 steps) in the next 30 min** (p=.06);
  effect decayed with exposure; anti-sedentary suggestions did less.
  https://www.researchgate.net/publication/327451781
- **HeartSteps II** ran 90 days with an RL agent personalizing delivery;
  methodological literature, no large distal-outcome win published.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35206455/
- **JustWalk JITAI (n=48, 270 days):** system-ID study of need /
  opportunity / receptivity states + goals varied from ~baseline toward
  8,000; results feed models, not a headline effect.
  https://www.researchprotocols.org/2023/1/e52161

**Reading:** context-timed prompts nudge the next half hour, not the
month. The durable lever is the adaptive GOAL + a visible gap ("2,100
left"), not prompt volume. Cap prompts hard; habituation is measured.

## 3. Post-meal walking — the best-kept honest trick

- **Glucose: PROVEN.** Buffey 2022 meta (7 crossover studies): light
  walking after meals blunts postprandial glucose vs sitting; even 2-5
  min helps; standing helps less. Peak-glucose reductions ~17-24%
  vs sitting across studies; 10-min immediate walks lower 2-h AUC; the
  30-90 min post-meal window is the effective zone; benefit saturates
  ~15-20 min. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8912639/
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-07312-y
  https://www.news-medical.net/health/Walking-After-Meals-Small-Habit-Big-Metabolic-Gains.aspx
- **Appetite/weight via post-meal walks: CLAIMED.** No trial shows the
  habit changes weight by itself; acute walking bouts do not reliably
  suppress (or raise) intake. Frame as steadier energy/glucose +
  digestion, never as a burn.
- **GLP-1 relevance:** gentle post-meal movement is commonly advised for
  GLP-1 fullness/sluggish digestion (mechanistically sensible; no RCT —
  CLAIMED). A post-meal walk is also the lowest-friction VILPA/NEAT entry
  for a deconditioned user.

**Verdict: a post-meal walk prompt is evidence-backed (glucose), cheap,
and uniquely Jeni** — the food log gives the trigger moment no fitness
app has. "a 10-minute walk after your biggest meal keeps glucose
steadier" is honest and provenance-clean.

## 4. Sedentary interruption — physiology yes, nudges maybe

- Acute crossover trials: breaking sitting improves glucose/BP; every
  30 min × 5 min light walking is the strongest dose for glycemia; even
  1 min/hour moves BP. PROVEN acutely.
  https://www.sedentarybehaviour.org/2023/05/31/breaking-up-prolonged-sitting-to-improve-cardiometabolic-risk-dose-response-analysis-of-a-randomized-crossover-trial/
  https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/sms.14769
- Prompt interventions (meta of workplace RCTs): −12.5 min sitting/workday,
  +1,030 steps/workday; GRADE low-moderate; ~60-min prompt frequency looks
  optimal; 12-week app trial cut occupational sitting ~70 min/day.
  PROMISING for behavior; long-term health outcomes from nudges: CLAIMED.
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12966-025-01781-0
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7764765/
- UX reality: Apple's hourly stand nag is among the most-disabled,
  most-mocked notifications on the platform; "how to turn off" is a
  content genre. https://www.laptopmag.com/news/how-to-turn-off-stand-reminders-apple-watch

**Verdict:** a generic hourly "you've been still" is CONVENTION Apple
already owns and users already resent. If Jeni ever surfaces stillness,
it must be rare, pattern-derived ("your afternoons run still lately" as
an observation), or fused with the post-meal moment — never an hourly
metronome. Phone-only detection of sitting is also weak (phone ≠ body).

## 5. Strength on GLP-1 — the one "workout" with a reason to exist

### 5.1 The problem — PROVEN
Lean/fat-free mass is 25-40% of GLP-1 weight loss (STEP-1 DXA subset:
~39-40%; pooled reads 26-40%). SEMALEAN and successors confirm lean loss;
function loss is more contested.
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/2050313X251388724
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12673431/

And the behavior compounds it: in the first wearable-linked cohort (All
of Us, n=753, ENDO 2026), users DROPPED from **5,047 → 4,487 steps/day**
and 28 → 22 MVPA min/day after starting a GLP-1; no evidence weight loss
made them move more. PROMISING (observational, first of kind).
https://www.endocrine.org/news-and-advocacy/news-room/2026/maharjan-press-release-endo-2026
https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/06/260614011841.htm

### 5.2 The fix — resistance training + protein
- During caloric restriction generally, resistance training preserves
  fat-free mass: PROVEN (decades of metas). During GLP-1 therapy
  specifically: PROMISING — S-LiTE (liraglutide ± supervised exercise,
  1 yr) showed exercise+drug lost the most fat while preserving muscle
  and fitness; LEAN-PREP (semaglutide/tirzepatide × RT × protein RCT,
  n=232) is in the field; case series show preservation with RT 3-5
  d/wk + protein. The "20-40% → 5-10% lean share with RT" figure
  circulating in industry copy is extrapolated, not GLP-1-trial-proven:
  CLAIMED. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34487446/
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13110620/
- Protein: expert consensus (Obesity Pillars 2025) prioritizes protein
  during pharmacological loss — synergy with Jeni's protein floor bar.
- **Minimum effective dose — PROVEN for strength:** a single set of 4+
  exercises to high effort, **2×/week, ~70-85% load, 6-12 reps** improves
  strength in trained and untrained adults (Sports Med 2024 minimal-dose
  overview; 1RM meta). Hypertrophy scales with more volume, but the
  floor is real and small: two 20-30 min sessions/week.
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40279-024-02009-0
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31797219/

### 5.3 Who delivers this in-app today
- **Noom Move "Muscle Defense"** — curated GLP-1 movement program; Noom's
  GLP-1 Companion added dynamic calorie goals + side-effect support.
  https://markets.financialcontent.com/borgernewsherald/article/gnwcq-2024-5-9
- **MyNetDiary GLP-1 Companion** (May 2026) — protein focus + injection
  reminders, no real training layer.
  https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mynetdiary-launches-glp-1-companion-for-ozempic-wegovy-and-mounjaro-users-302761158.html
- **Omada GLP-1 Care Track**, WeightWatchers GLP-1 program — coaching
  wrappers; movement guidance is generic.
- Gyms (Equinox et al., ~17 chains) run GLP-1 muscle programs; Peloton
  positions strength-for-GLP-1 content. Nobody owns "the 40-minute weekly
  strength floor for the medicated" in a quiet, non-gym register. That
  slot is open.

## 6. Movement formats for the deconditioned — what fits Jeni

- **Walking programs:** the modality of the evidence base and of NWCR
  maintainers; zero equipment, zero identity threat. The wedge.
- **Exercise snacks / VILPA:** 3× ~1-2 min vigorous bursts/day associate
  with −38-40% all-cause mortality in non-exercisers (PROMISING,
  wearable cohorts); stair-snack RCTs improve VO2peak modestly (PROVEN,
  small); meta of ≤5-min bouts 2×/day improves CRF in inactive adults.
  Great fallback actions for zero-momentum days.
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-022-02100-x
  https://cdnsciencepub.com/doi/10.1139/apnm-2018-0675
  https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/cardiovascular-medicine/articles/10.3389/fcvm.2025.1643153/full
- **Pilates/mobility:** meta shows Pilates helps weight/body-fat slightly
  in overweight populations and adherence in short trials is decent, but
  it is a class-content arms race (Alo, Lotus, YouTube) and not
  provenance-measurable by phone. PROMISING for engagement, weak for
  weight; content production ≠ Jeni's edge.
  https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33776797/
- **Jeni's realistic wedge:** movement as *support for the medicated and
  deconditioned* — adaptive walking + post-meal walks + a 2×/week
  strength floor + snack-sized fallbacks. Complement the drug and the
  food log; never compete on workout libraries (legacy sessions already
  proved it: ~23% engagement vs 75% lessons/breath).

## 7. Retention teardowns

- **Gentler Streak** (Apple Watch App of the Year 2022): "Activity Path"
  = a moving readiness band from her own trend; rest is prescribed as
  progress; counts dog walks and chores; no fixed goals, no shame.
  Users explicitly migrate from ring anxiety to it. The register Jeni
  already speaks — proof the gentle frame retains.
  https://www.sketch.com/blog/gentler-streak/
  https://neura.health/insight/gentler-streak-app-hands-on-review
- **Apple rings:** brilliant completion loop, documented dark side —
  streak anxiety, fake workouts while ill, exercising through injury;
  Apple retrofitted pauses + per-day goals in 2024. Lesson: closed-shape
  completion works; *unbroken-chain pressure* is the harm vector.
  https://www.techradar.com/health-fitness/smartwatches/my-apple-watch-is-going-to-hate-this
  https://trophy.so/blog/the-psychology-of-apple-watchs-close-your-rings
- **WeWard** (cash-for-steps): streak flames + validation loops + ~$10
  per 100 days of 10k steps; retention via micro-payment variable reward.
  Made users walk 24% more — but it is an ads/partnership business whose
  currency is attention. For a paid clinical-grade product: GIMMICK;
  extrinsic rewards also decay when payments stop (standard incentive
  literature). https://www.wewardapp.com/blog/how-we-built-the-streak-feature-to-boost-user-retention-and-create-healthy-habits
  https://www.cnbc.com/2024/12/18/this-app-pays-you-to-hit-your-daily-step-countearn-up-to-1000/year.html
- **StepsApp:** pure pedometer utility — widgets, watch faces, goal
  confetti; retention rides glanceability, not coaching. Lesson: the
  count itself, always visible, is the product for many; Jeni gets the
  same via the Today tile without needing another app's shell.
- **Pacer:** streaks with 2 built-in rest days/week — forgiveness as a
  first-class rule, matching §2.2.

## 8. Apple Health integration — what's real phone-only

- **Steps (phone-only): reliable enough.** iPhone vs ActivPAL r≈0.93
  (watch r≈0.99); ~3-5% error carried; real failure mode = not carrying
  the phone (undercount), so daily totals skew low at home.
  Design consequence: adaptive goals from HER OWN phone baseline
  self-correct for carry habits; never compare her to watch-wearers.
  https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6995435/
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6329418/
- **Sleep (no watch): weak.** iPhone alone yields schedule-anchored
  time-in-bed inference (motion + screen/DND cues), no stages. Rise
  built a business on "tappigraphy" instead of pretending. Any Jeni
  sleep feature must degrade gracefully to "went to bed late / short
  night" granularity — never render stages it cannot see.
  https://www.risescience.com/blog/best-sleep-app-tracker-without-a-watch
- **Active energy: unreliable.** ~28% mean error across 56 studies
  (worse for larger bodies — exactly Jeni's cohort). Never convert
  movement to kcal on-surface; it also violates the provenance law.
  https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1361-6579/adca82
- **Workout auto-detection:** a Watch feature (HR-based), not phone.
  Phone-only Jeni can read HealthKit workouts *written by other apps or
  a watch* and should absorb them silently ("you already moved today"
  → no walking action), but cannot auto-recognize sessions itself.
  CMPedometer cadence/distance is available for walk-detection heuristics
  (10 continuous brisk minutes is detectable phone-in-pocket).

## 9. Sleep — big lever, small surface

### 9.1 Causal chain to intake — PROVEN
Experimental sleep restriction raises intake **+204 to +385 kcal/day**
(metas of RCTs), raises hunger, ghrelin, sweet preference, and reward
response to food; restriction under free-living conditions adds weight
in weeks. Short sleep is a food-noise amplifier — mechanistically the
exact enemy Jeni fights.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30870662/
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7071396/

### 9.2 Extension helps — PROMISING (one strong RCT)
Tasali 2022 (JAMA IM, n=80 short-sleeping adults with overweight,
real-life setting): a single counseling session extended sleep +1.2
h/night and cut intake **−270 kcal/day** vs control over 2 weeks —
no diet advice at all. Replication pending; effect size is the punchline:
sleep is worth roughly a snack of willpower per night.
https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamainternalmedicine/fullarticle/2788694

### 9.3 Sleep predicts weight-loss success — PROMISING
In behavioral WL programs, baseline short/poor sleep → less loss
(−4.5% at 12 mo for short sleepers; each extra baseline hour ≈ +0.82%
loss; short+poor sleepers did worst) and lower adherence. Sleep state is
a legitimate *pattern input* for why a week ran hard.
https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/15402002.2026.2648510
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41366-022-01141-z

### 9.4 Regularity beats duration — PROMISING
UK Biobank (n>60k): sleep *regularity* predicts mortality better than
duration (−20-48% across quintiles). The actionable unit is a consistent
bedtime window — which phone signals CAN observe — not stage
architecture, which they can't.
https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/47/1/zsad253/7280269

### 9.5 How trackers convert sleep to behavior + the failure mode
Rise reduces everything to two numbers (sleep debt, circadian timing)
and one behavior (wind-down at the right time); Oura/WHOOP sell
readiness scores that mostly feed dashboards. Meanwhile **orthosomnia**
(Baron 2017, JCSM) documents data-driven sleep perfectionism worsening
sleep — a live risk for Jeni's anxiety-prone cohort and the exact
opposite of the anti-shame law.
https://www.risescience.com/blog/best-sleep-debt-tracking-app
https://www.sleepfoundation.org/orthosomnia

### 9.6 Verdict on surface area
Sleep earns: (1) one observed fact when notable ("short night — food
noise runs louder today"), (2) a wind-down/bedtime-consistency anchor
as an occasional evening action, (3) input into the pattern engine
(timing-never-causality, same as v24). Sleep does NOT earn: a score, a
stage chart, a nightly report, or a tile empire. It is a modifier of
the food story, not its own product.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR JENI (ranked)

1. **BUILD — Adaptive walking action on Today.** Goal = ~60th percentile
   of her own last 9-10 days (Adams recipe), floor ~2,500, ceiling ~8,000,
   recalc daily, breathes down after bad weeks. Surface as the gap, not
   the total: "2,100 steps left" when within reach. This is the
   best-evidenced behavioral mechanic in the whole domain (+~1,000
   steps/day vs static goals; 58% vs 22% attainment) and phone-only
   reliable. Never default to 10,000 — it is a 1965 pedometer slogan.

2. **BUILD — Post-meal walk as a contextual action.** The food log gives
   Jeni the trigger no fitness app has. "10 gentle minutes after your
   biggest meal" — glucose framing only (PROVEN), digestion comfort for
   the medicated (CLAIMED, say it softly), never calories. This is the
   single most differentiated movement feature available.

3. **BUILD — The strength floor for the medication cohort.** 25-40% of
   GLP-1 loss is lean mass and users' steps DROP after starting (5,047 →
   4,487). Two 20-30 min sessions/week, 4-6 movements, one hard set each,
   paired with the protein floor — "keep what you built" register.
   This is the only workout content with a reason to exist in Jeni, and
   the in-app slot (quiet, non-gym, medication-aware) is genuinely open.
   Log-only + guided-text first; no video production arms race.

4. **BUILD — GLP-1 movement-decline watch.** Pattern engine hook: steps
   sliding after a dose change is an observed, cohort-documented risk —
   "your steps picked down after the dose changed" fits v24's
   timing-never-causality grammar exactly.

5. **BUILD (small) — Movement snacks as fallback.** 2-5 min options
   ("one flight, three times today") for zero-momentum days; VILPA
   evidence lets Jeni honestly say tiny counts. Keeps the 1-3 daily
   actions issuable even on the worst day.

6. **BUILD (minimal) — Sleep as modifier, not surface.** Bedtime-window
   consistency nudge (regularity evidence) + short-night → next-day
   appetite fact (restriction evidence) + pattern-engine input (sleep
   predicts adherence). Phone-only truthfully sees only timing/duration
   — render nothing finer. The Tasali result (−270 kcal from +1.2h) is
   the one sleep number worth teaching.

7. **ADOPT — Relief mechanics as law.** Goals breathe down; 1-2
   effort-free days/week are structural (Pacer), not exceptions; any
   consistency object repairs rather than resets (two-day rule). Gentler
   Streak proves the register retains; Apple rings prove unbroken-chain
   pressure eventually sheds the exact users Jeni serves.

8. **ABSORB — HealthKit workouts from elsewhere.** If a watch/app wrote a
   workout, Today's movement action auto-completes. Jeni never asks for
   what the phone already knows (passive law).

### DO-NOT-BUILD
- **A workout library / Nike Training Club-lite.** 23% legacy engagement,
  saturated market, zero wedge. Strength floor (item 3) is the only
  exception, and it is a protocol, not a library.
- **10,000-step default or any fixed universal goal.** Marketing artifact;
  static goals demonstrably underperform adaptive ones.
- **Calorie-burn display from active energy.** ~28% device error, worst
  for larger bodies; violates provenance; invites burn/earn framing.
- **Sleep score, stage charts, nightly sleep report.** Phone can't see
  stages; scores breed orthosomnia; clutter against L-law minimalism.
- **Hourly stand/stillness nags.** Apple owns it and users disable it;
  at most a rare pattern observation.
- **Cash/points-for-steps.** Wrong business model, extrinsic-reward decay,
  wrong register.
- **Hard-reset streaks.** Documented harm vector for this cohort.
- **GPS route tracking.** The behavior needs a count, not a map; battery
  + privacy cost with no evidence payoff.
