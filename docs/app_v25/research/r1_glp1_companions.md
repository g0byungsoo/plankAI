# R1 — GLP-1 COMPANION APPS AND THEIR USERS (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-08-10. Domain: what GLP-1 users actually do, want, abandon,
and pay for in companion apps; where every current product fails.
Labels: **PROVEN** (evidence/replicated) · **PROMISING** (credible early) ·
**CLAIMED** (marketing) · **CONVENTION** (category habit) · **GIMMICK**.

A hygiene note up front: this category's discovery layer is polluted. The
"#1 GLP-1 app" headlines for MeAgain are paid press-release wires
([financialcontent](https://markets.financialcontent.com/wral/article/abnewswire-2025-12-8-meagain-ranked-the-1-glp-1-tracking-app-for-2026),
[barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36027020/meagain-named-the-1-zepbound-tirzepatide-tracking-app-for-weight-management-success)),
and most "best of" listicles are content marketing by adjacent vendors
([gila.coach](https://gila.coach/learn/best-glp1-tracking-apps-compared-2026) sells habit coaching,
[donedose.com](https://www.donedose.com/guides/best-glp1-tracker-app) ranks itself). Treat rankings as CLAIMED; user reviews and Reddit-derived
synthesis as the real signal.

## 3. WHAT USERS TRACK — AND REFUSE TO TRACK

- Track willingly: **shot day + site, weight trend, protein, water, side effects,
  progress photos**. CONVENTION across every product; matches shot-day ritual culture
  ([askglo](https://www.askglo.ai/blog/glp-1-shot-day-routine)).
- The **medication-level curve is the most emotionally engaging artifact in the
  category** — users describe it as "fascinating," check it daily, use it to explain
  their week ([glp1effect](https://glp1effect.com/p/are-glp-1-app-medication-charts-reliable)). PROVEN engagement, disputed science (§10).
- **Food noise is becoming a tracked vital.** MeAgain made it a named 0-10 slider;
  clinical formalization of the term in 2025; users self-report day-5-to-7 return
  ([meagain.com/glp-1-food-noise](https://meagain.com/glp-1-food-noise),
  [thegentleplate](https://www.thegentleplate.com/food-noise-returns/)). PROMISING → soon CONVENTION.
- Refuse / abandon: **full calorie bookkeeping.** 50-70% abandon food logging within two
  weeks (category stat, [mywhy.net](https://mywhy.net/blog/glp1-companion-app-guide));
  on-GLP-1 specifically, the **half-portion problem** — "I'm constantly editing serving
  sizes to a third and it's exhausting"; databases assume full servings, so manual logs
  over-count and "hide whether the patient is hitting protein at all"
  ([clinicalnutritionreport](https://clinicalnutritionreport.com/articles/best-glp1-app-reddit-2026/)). PROVEN pain.
  (That same article's "PlateLens" consensus claim reads as astroturf — treat the
  complaint as real, the recommended app as unverified.)
- Refuse: shame surfaces. Users keep the med private even from family; apps that never
  name the drug in notifications are valued
  ([Klarity privacy](https://www.helloklarity.com/post/your-glp-1-privacy-why-keeping-your-weight-loss-journey-private-can-be-empowering/)). CONVENTION among the good indies (Shotsy's neutral notifications).

## 4. DOSE LOGISTICS (the anxiety map)

- **The weekly cycle is the product.** Day 1-2 peak (appetite gone, nausea risk),
  day 3-5 "sweet spot," day 6-7 hunger + food noise return; shot day anticipated "with
  relief or even excitement" ([Klarity](https://www.helloklarity.com/post/the-glp-1-weekly-cycle-understanding-your-bodys-response-to-weight-loss-medication/),
  [Russ Powell](https://www.russpowellnutrition.com/blog/why-hunger-returns-before-your-glp-1-shot-and-how-to-fix-it)). PROVEN lived pattern.
- **Titration anxiety is real and mood-linked**: users report anxiety/mood symptoms
  spiking after dose increases, easing when holding — "It got noticeably worse when I
  went from 7.5mg to 10mg… I'm holding the dose for now"; community norm = slow
  titration, stay longer at lower doses
  ([medino](https://www.medino.com/article/mounjaro-and-mood)). PROMISING (self-report).
- **Missed/late dose rules are drug-specific and users don't know them**: Zepbound
  4-day rule (take if <4 days late, schedule shifts; never two doses within 72h);
  Wegovy 48-hour rule with a 5-day override nuance
  ([formblends](https://formblends.com/articles/glp1-hub/missed-dose-of-zepbound-what-to-do-and-when-it-still-works),
  [pillo.care](https://pillo.care/blog/missed-dose-of-wegovy),
  [healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health/drugs/missed-dose-zepbound)). No major app encodes these at log time. GAP.
- **Travel**: storage rules vary per product (fridge vs room-temp windows), TSA +
  sharps container logistics, dose-timing planning before trips
  ([askglo](https://www.askglo.ai/blog/glp-1-shot-day-routine)). Apps ignore it. GAP.
- **Pens vs vials vs pills**: LillyDirect pushed single-dose **vials** (syringe draw —
  new technique burden) at $299-499 ([Lilly](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-additional-zepbound-vial-doses-and-offers-new));
  Wegovy pill has empty-stomach/water constraints, Foundayo has none
  ([Pharmacy Times](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-approves-orforglipron-first-glp-1-pill-without-time-food-or-water-restrictions));
  Shotsy alone logs pill fasting/water context. Mixed-mode regimens (pill + injectable
  history) are now normal. PROVEN.
- **Compounded reality post-shortage**: shortage declared over Feb 21 2025; 503A
  compounding stopped Apr 22, 503B May 22 2025
  ([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-alerts-and-statements/fda-clarifies-policies-compounders-national-glp-1-supply-begins-stabilize),
  [Pharmacy Times](https://www.pharmacytimes.com/view/fda-moves-to-permanently-close-the-door-on-compounded-glp-1s)).
  455+ adverse-event reports, many multidose-vial dosing errors
  ([MNT](https://www.medicalnewstoday.com/articles/is-compounded-semaglutide-still-available)).
  Displaced users went to brand self-pay — or the **gray market** (268 kg of bulk
  semaglutide imported 2023-24 ≈ millions of doses; formaldehyde contamination found;
  uninspected Chinese suppliers) ([Brookings](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/the-wild-east-of-semaglutide/)). PROVEN.
- **Microdosing/stretching**: 15% of 8,000+ surveyed users tried it, **half without
  telling their doctor** — drivers: cost, side effects; AACE does not support it
  ([STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/29/glp-1-microdose-popular-but-unsupported-by-evidence/),
  [WTOP](https://wtop.com/news/2026/07/glp-1-microdosing-risks-benefits-and-what-to-know-before-changing-your-dose/)). PROVEN behavior, unsupported practice.
- **Insurance-driven switches are traumatic**: CVS Caremark dropped Zepbound July 1 2025
  → forced Wegovy switches for millions → "approved two months ago, already lost
  25.8 lbs" backlash → CVS reversed under pressure
  ([Fox](https://www.foxnews.com/health/weight-loss-patients-taking-zepbound-forced-switch-wegovy-after-drop-coverage),
  [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/health-news/cvs-caremark-will-cover-lillys-weight-loss-drug-zepbound-rcna347253),
  [On The Pen](https://onthepen.substack.com/p/cvs-is-dropping-zepbound-here-is)). PROVEN: regimens change for
  non-medical reasons; users fear "losing the med that finally worked."
- **Stopping = regain**: SURMOUNT-4-style data — ~14% regain within a year of stopping
  tirzepatide ([Reuters via AOL](https://www.aol.com/news/patients-lillys-zepbound-regain-weight-155530308.html)). PROVEN. Fuels §7.

## 5. SIDE EFFECTS — struggle, self-care, and the reporting gap

- Clinically expected: nausea, constipation, fatigue dominate. Community self-care
  canon: small meals, avoid fatty/greasy food, hydration, fiber, ginger, protein-first
  ([Klarity](https://www.helloklarity.com/post/the-glp-1-weekly-cycle-understanding-your-bodys-response-to-weight-loss-medication/),
  [AOL/Hers tips](https://www.aol.com/lifestyle/7-tips-reduce-tirzepatide-nausea-115630811.html)). CONVENTION.
- **The underreported set (UPenn AI analysis of 400K+ Reddit posts): menstrual
  irregularities, temperature dysregulation (chills/hot flashes), fatigue (#2 real-world
  complaint), psychiatric symptoms** — all far above clinical-trial reporting
  ([CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/health/article/thousands-are-sharing-side-effects-of-weight-loss-drugs-doctors-may-miss-study-suggests/),
  [Doctronic](https://www.doctronic.ai/blog/glp-1-side-effects-that-clinical-trials-missed/)). PROVEN gap: apps track the trial list, not the lived list.
- **Why patients under-report: "they didn't want to complain because they were losing
  weight — and wound up in the ER with extreme constipation or a small bowel
  obstruction"** ([MDedge](https://community.the-hospitalist.org/clinicalendocrinologynews/article/271689/obesity/side-effects-glp-1-drugs-what-doctors-should-know)). PROVEN clinical quote; the single best argument for passive symptom capture.
- **Hair**: >75% of GLP-1 initiators report new/worsening shedding (cross-sectional,
  [J Cosmetic Derm 2026](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jocd.70835)); 37%
  higher alopecia risk vs SGLT-2i in TriNetX cohort
  ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666328726000180));
  mechanism = telogen effluvium from rapid loss + nutrient shortfall (iron, protein,
  zinc); typically self-limiting 3-6 mo ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/11/14/health/glp-1-hair-loss-telogen-effluvium-wellness),
  [Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health-news/glp-1-drugs-may-cause-hair-loss-treatment)). PROVEN, and almost no app tracks it (gila flagged "hair shedding" as a missing signal).
- Hydration/protein/fiber guidance demand is universal — every serious companion now
  ships protein/fiber/water goals (MeAgain, Noom, Shotsy via Health import). CONVENTION.

## 6. FOOD RELATIONSHIP ON GLP-1

- Appetite collapse breaks calorie-tracker assumptions (half-portion problem, §3). PROVEN.
- **Muscle-loss fear is the #1 nutrition anxiety**: 25-39% of weight lost can be
  fat-free mass without intervention ([clinicalnutritionreport](https://clinicalnutritionreport.com/articles/best-glp1-app-reddit-2026/));
  press coverage pushes protein targets ~0.45-0.68 g/lb
  ([Healthline](https://www.healthline.com/health-news/protein-muscle-loss-weight-loss-drugs)).
  Industry response: Noom "Muscle Defense," WW LIFTED + AI body scanner, protein-first
  logging everywhere. PROVEN fear, CLAIMED solutions.
- Food aversion/nausea triggers: users want to identify which foods went bad for them
  (fatty/greasy top the list). Apps do nothing structured here. GAP.
- **Alcohol quietly drops**: 72% of 1,500+ analyzed Reddit posts referenced reduced
  craving/interest ([PMC meta](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12663662/));
  RCT support emerging ([NIH](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/glp-1-plus-therapy-can-reduce-heavy-drinking)). PROVEN signal; no companion tracks or celebrates it. GAP.
- Eating becomes logistical ("must hit protein in 900 kcal") and social eating gets
  easier mid-cycle, harder late-cycle ([Klarity](https://www.helloklarity.com/post/the-glp-1-weekly-cycle-understanding-your-bodys-response-to-weight-loss-medication/)). PROMISING framing for meal support.

## 7. MAINTENANCE AND OFF-RAMPING

- Rebound fear is rational: ~14% regain after stopping tirzepatide within a year
  ([AOL/Reuters](https://www.aol.com/news/patients-lillys-zepbound-regain-weight-155530308.html)); regain discourse saturates the subs. PROVEN.
- What exists: **Shotsy Maintenance Mode (Mar 2026, category first)**; WW "Maintain
  Mode"; nothing else. The entire category is architected for the loss phase only
  ([soft112](https://shotsy-glp-1-tracker-ios.soft112.com/),
  [hitconsultant](https://hitconsultant.net/2025/12/17/weight-watchers-launches-new-glp-1-program-and-ai-app-features/)). GAP with first-movers only.
- What users do in reality: spacing doses out (10-14 days), dropping to lower dose,
  microdosing — half without prescriber sign-off ([STAT](https://www.statnews.com/2026/05/29/glp-1-microdose-popular-but-unsupported-by-evidence/)). PROVEN behavior no app acknowledges.
- Demand: reassurance structure — "what does my body do between doses," early-warning
  weight/food-noise drift, strength preservation, identity beyond the drug
  ([gila.coach](https://gila.coach/learn/best-glp1-tracking-apps-compared-2026) frames it as the unbuilt "after the tracker" layer). PROMISING.

## 8. LOVE / ABANDON / TEDIOUS / REQUEST / PAY

- **LOVE**: the med-level curve ("fascinating"); one-app consolidation ("I used to
  juggle 4 different apps. This one app now does it all"
  ([Globe/MeAgain PR](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/markets/markets-news/GetNews/35130773/best-glp1-app-for-2025-meagain-takes-top-position/), user-quoted));
  the food-noise-vs-curve aha ([meagain.com](https://meagain.com/glp-1-food-noise));
  appointment-ready summaries ([shotsyapp.com](https://shotsyapp.com/)); shot-day ritual
  support; privacy-respecting design.
- **ABANDON**: manual food logging within ~2 weeks ([mywhy](https://mywhy.net/blog/glp1-companion-app-guide));
  apps that gate basics ("can't track ANYTHING without the membership" —
  [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/meagain-glp-1-all-in-one-app/6744178534)); telehealth apps after
  leaving the med program; pet-gamification once novelty fades (GIMMICK decay).
- **TEDIOUS**: serving-size fractioning; re-entering the same breakfast; duplicative
  weight entry when a smart scale exists; navigating app + pharmacy + PBM portals.
- **REQUEST (repeatedly)**: free trial before subscription; Android parity (indies are
  iOS-first); smart scale/Oura/CGM integrations; carb/calorie visibility even on
  GLP-1-specific apps; faster support; photo-gallery polish ([mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/meagain-glp-1-all-in-one-app/6744178534)).
- **PAY**: AI photo food logging, med-level charts (premium gate in MeAgain/GlucoPal/

## 9. THE PRESCRIBER GAP (between-visit reality)

- Patients systematically under-report: GI severity (until ER), menstrual/temperature/
  psychiatric symptoms (not on the checklist), microdosing and dose-stretching (~50%
  hidden), gray-market sourcing (§4-5). PROVEN across sources.
- What they wish prescribers knew: the late-week hunger window; which side effects map
  to which dose change; that mood shifted after titration; what they can no longer eat;
  cost pressure driving skipped fills.
- Current bridge: **PDF export (Shotsy) — "feel prepared at every appointment."**
  Telehealth-attached apps have async messaging but reviews show slow response (Found,
  Hims). GAP: no consumer companion turns logs into a structured between-visit signal a
  clinician can act on. **PDURS** (Noom's bet) is the regulatory route by which such a
  companion could end up referenced on the drug label
  ([Noom/PDURS](https://markets.financialcontent.com/custercountychief/article/gnwcq-2025-1-14-nooms-glp-1-companion-receives-major-update-to-prepare-for-new-expected-prescription-drug-use-related-software-pdurs-model)). PROMISING strategic runway for a clinic-connected product.

## 10. WHERE CURRENT COMPANIONS FAIL (specific)

- **Shotsy**: no behavior/coaching layer; food is an import, not an experience; PK chart
  educates but can mislead (below).
- **MeAgain**: hard paywall on basics + weekly-subscription billing anger; AI food scan
  inconsistency; engagement mascot ≠ care; PR-manufactured credibility.
- **PK curves everywhere (Shotsy/Glapp/GLP-1 Plotter/GLPeak)**: clinical pharmacologist:
  "You may find it fun to plot concentration but it's fun only and scientifically
  inaccurate" — time-to-peak varies 8-72h, 2-3× interindividual concentration variation,
  no concentration↔effect mapping
  ([glp1effect](https://glp1effect.com/p/are-glp-1-app-medication-charts-reliable)). The category's most-loved feature is its least honest. PROVEN tension.
- **GlucoPal/Pep/Pokii/GLP Buddy**: single-strength tools; no arc, no maintenance, tiny teams.
- **Ro/Found/Noom Med/Zealthy/Hims**: app loyalty is hostage to the med subscription;
  billing/cancellation is the #1 complaint class; Hims adds an FTC data-sharing suit —
  trust is the category's open wound.
- **WW Med+**: brand-new post-bankruptcy promises (body scanner, Weight Health Score)
  — unproven, and WW's track record is program-first, product-second.
- **Everyone**: no travel/timezone help, no missed-dose rules at the moment of logging,
  no insurance-switch support, no maintenance depth (one exception), no tracking of the
  underreported symptom set, no acknowledgment of real user behavior (stretching,
  stacking, gray market) — the apps describe an idealized patient.

## IMPLICATIONS FOR JENI (ranked, opinionated)

Jeni v24 already has the platform the indies lack: regimen version chains, deterministic
dose events, rotation that suggests, timing-never-causality patterns, an actionable
reminder category, consult beats, clinic mode. The gap between Jeni and this field is
architecture; the gap between Jeni and users is coverage of the moments below.

1. **Own the weekly cycle honestly (highest leverage).** The PK curve is the category's
   most-loved artifact and it is pseudo-quantitative. Jeni's provenance law forbids the
   curve — good. Ship the honest substitute: cycle-day framing on Today/Regimen home
   ("day 6 of 7 — appetite often returns about now; nothing is wrong") + food-noise as
   a first-class 0-10 log tied to dose timing in the pattern engine. MeAgain proved the
   aha ("made it make so much sense"); Jeni can deliver it without fake precision.
2. **Encode missed/late-dose label rules at log time.** Zepbound 4-day / 72h-minimum,
   Wegovy 48h/5-day — surfaced as label facts when a dose is late, never as advice.
   Nobody does this; it is the single most Googled panic moment. Fits the reminder
   category's "log later" path and the observed-never-prescribed law.
3. **Build the maintenance era before the market does.** Only Shotsy (Mar 2026) has
   anything. Jeni's era ledger + version chains are a natural "maintenance era": goal
   reached → cadence support shifts to protein/strength/drift-watch; regain fear met
   with trend honesty. This is also the retention answer — the tracker that survives
   the prescription outlives the category.
4. **Between-visit consult prep.** Turn the dose/symptom/food record into a one-page
   "bring to your appointment" summary (consumer) and the clinic-mode live equivalent
   (B2B). Shotsy's PDF is loved; Jeni's care platform can make it a connection instead
   of a printout. PDURS is the long-run prize for exactly this shape.
5. **Track the underreported symptom set.** Add fatigue, hair shedding, menstrual
   changes, temperature, mood to the side-effect vocabulary (timing-never-causality
   framing already protects compliance). Differentiator backed by the 400K-post study;
   no competitor covers it.
6. **Kill tracking friction GLP-1-specifically.** The half-portion problem is Jeni's
   home turf: photo-first logging already measures the plate; add "ate about a third"
   fractioning on the reading and protein-adequacy framing (floor bar exists). Never
   make her fraction database servings.
7. **Survive the switch.** Insurance/PBM whiplash (CVS-Zepbound) and med switches are
   normal life events: make "new medication / new era" a first-class beat on the
   version chain — record the why (insurance, side effects, cost), reset expectations
   gently, never imply equivalence between drugs (compliance floor).
8. **Travel beat (small, real).** Pre-trip: storage window facts per product, sharps
   note, reminder stability across timezones (schedule engine is already wall-clock;
   surface it as a promise: "your Tuesday shot stays Tuesday").
9. **Celebrate the quiet wins.** Alcohol interest dropping (72% of posts) and food-noise
   quiet are the felt benefits users evangelize; log-lightly + reflect ("drinks haven't
   appeared in three weeks") in the NSV voice Jeni already has.
