- Aha ≤5 min → ~40% higher 30-day retention (Reforge). 82% of trial starts = same session as install; 55% of cancellations on Day 0.
- RevenueCat Noom teardown names Jeni's exact seam: after 113 screens + payment "I still don't know what the app experience actually looks like."
- RANKED PATTERNS: 1) guided first REAL action in minute one (Duolingo lesson-before-signup +20% DAU; Cal AI logging within 2 min; MacroFactor's setup-before-first-log = overwhelming). 2) first record must exist AND be ANSWERED in session one (Headspace magic moment; MFP answers with computed target). 3) day-one contract with payout date (Whoop gray recovery score, 4-day calibration promise; Blinkist charge-date honesty). 4) endowed-progress checklist ONLY AFTER first value. 5) empty states teach the verb not the void ("an empty state that feels filled"). 6) progressive disclosure keyed to STATE not time. 7) teach correction AT the first result (Cal AI's corrections don't persist → users stop correcting → trust dies). 8) post-paywall bridge: restate what she bought in her numbers → one action.
- ANTI-PATTERNS: multi-step tour · tooltip storm · unskippable celebration blocking the record (Noom verbatim) · setup-before-value · post-purchase cliff · upsell in first paid session · fake fullness (seeded fake data) · first-action that can fail without fallback (MFP "searched 'egg', no options") · correction taught in docs or corrections that don't stick.
