# AGENT EXTRACT — body/water/movement evidence (acc2351ed)

## BODY SNAP
Photo BF% validation: Amazon Halo npj 2022 (n=134): MAE 2.16–2.34%, but 95% LoA −5.5..+4.7 pts, test-retest ±1.6 pts, company-authored. Shaped npj 2025 (n=1273): MAE 1.56–1.72%, CCC .98 — company-authored, tight-clothing-trained, bias at BMI≥30, no visceral/subQ. MeThreeSixty waist CV 0.93% ≈ pro 3D scanner (0.87%) but expert tape = 0.06%. ZOZOFIT: NO peer-reviewed DXA validation; BF% = US Navy circumference FORMULA. Change-sensitivity over weeks essentially unvalidated; week-over-week BF change < instrument noise (±1.6pt).
WAIST: IAS/ICCR consensus (Nat Rev Endo 2020): WC = routine vital sign, "reductions critically important treatment target"; women thresholds 80/90/105/115 by BMI class; NICE 2022 WHtR<0.5. WC is a FORMAL AOM trial endpoint: SELECT −7.7cm; SURMOUNT-5 −18.4 vs −13.0 cm. Self-measure: within 1–3 cm, ICC 0.97, 93% within ±10%; women underestimate, error largest with central adiposity; instruction video improves.
PROGRESS PHOTOS: adherence benefit = NOT PROVEN (no RCT; "60% more likely" = trainer marketing). HARM real: mirror-scrutiny experiments ↑ dissatisfaction; PLOS One 2024: increased body-checking week worsened drive-for-thinness/negative affect IN AT-RISK WOMEN (low-concern unaffected); body checking = transdiagnostic ED maintainer (2025 SR). Harm keys on FREQUENCY + evaluative framing. Fitness-app appearance-motivated users show more disordered eating; highest-risk stratum = college-educated women 18–29 = Jeni's demo.
USER SENTIMENT: ZOZOFIT 4.7★/7.4k: value named = "loses inches, not scale weight" (= GLP-1 plateau moment); complaints = capture ordeal ("Stand 6 feet away… 40+ min") + scan-to-scan noise ("up or down 2 inches from week before").
CAN claim: direction of silhouette change over WEEKS above noise floor; circumference-class trends ~1% CV with controlled capture; "inches moved when scale didn't". CANNOT: individual BF%; visceral/subQ; week-over-week change; any daily-cadence check; any health-risk claim.
VERDICT INPUTS: current shipped shape (weekly, words-never-numbers, 3% floor, never-scold, local-first) ≈ evidence-correct. ADD: optional SELF-TAPED WAIST trend (guideline-anointed, AOM-trial endpoint, cheap, no ML claims). Keep weekly cap (frequency = the harm dose). NEVER ship photo BF%.

## WATER
Premeal RCTs: Dennis 2010 (+2 kg) in AGE 55–75; mechanism FAILS in young (Van Walleghen 2007: reduced intake in older NOT younger); pooled 2024 meta (8 RCTs, Nutrients): −0.33 kg NS, WC NS. 8x8 = folklore (Valtin 2002); IOM: thirst suffices, 2.7 L includes food+caffeinated. GLP-1-specific: REAL but symptom-scoped — Wegovy label AKI warning via GI dehydration; STEP nausea 43.9%/diarrhea 29.7%/vomiting 24.5%/constipation 24.2%; Mayo Clin Proc 2025: small sips, fluids-before-fiber. User demand: obligation not delight (MFP: "absolutely no reason to log your water"); 64% delete apps sending 5+ notifs/wk — water reminders spend scarce budget on weakest evidence.
VERDICT INPUTS: DON'T build tracker/target/ring. Optional: symptom-scoped hydration notes on GLP-1 rail (= existing fluidsOnAQueasyDay pattern + dose-escalation weeks). Never a personal ml number without care-team source.

## MOVEMENT
Penetration: ~40% US 18-34 own smartwatch; 27.2% of women 25-34 → majority watchless but every iPhone gives steps. Manual-diary adherence drops ~50% after 7 days; passive PA monitoring = ONLY target whose adherence doesn't decay (93.6% stable vs eating 92.7→86.7, Obesity 2020).
Monitoring evidence: Burke 2011 — dietary self-monitoring most consistently predicts loss (15 studies); self-weighing (6); exercise monitoring: ONE study. Movement = context the app reads, not a logging behavior to demand.
Calories-earned: Stanford 7-device: EE error 27.4–92.6%, none acceptable; compensation eats 55–64% of predicted loss; MFP folk-law "eat back half". MacroFactor adherence-neutral = honest benchmark. Jeni's measured-or-absent law = evidence-aligned.
Resistance training: lean mass 25–39% of GLP-1 loss; RT 2–3×/wk reduces FFM loss 30–50% (2024 SR); joint advisory ≥3×/wk. Clinically meaningful unit = SESSIONS/WEEK vs 2–3 floor. No set-rep ledger (zero outcome evidence).
VERDICT INPUTS: auto-import first; manual door stays small (one-line); never calories-earned; RT = sessions/week tile.
