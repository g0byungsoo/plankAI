# v25 AUDIT — JENI AS A SYSTEM (2026-08-10)

The brief's ten questions applied to every major system. Inputs:
`audit/00_canon.md` (documented law + state), `audit/01_app_reality.md`
(code reality), `audit/03_walk_notes.md` (recorded walk),
`research/r1..r6` (category + science). Verdicts: **KEEP** (right as
built) · **IMPROVE** (right shape, widen/deepen) · **REBUILD** (the
problem is real, the form is wrong) · **REMOVE** (does not deserve to
exist) · **DEMOTE** (exists, loses its surface). Each verdict names the
evidence. "walk-confirm" marks items the recorded walk must verify.

The audit's compass (the brief): less user work, more intelligence.
A surface must answer one of the three questions (am i changing / why /
what should i do next) for someone specific, at a real frequency, or it
feeds an engine instead of owning pixels.

## 5. FOOD — KEEP the experience · IMPROVE the pipeline (flagship)

- Ten questions: highest-frequency surface (multiple/day), the
  strongest acquisition loop, the adaptive program's richest signal.
- v23 is the right product (one material story, one reading, the book).
  Do not rebuild the surface. The era's work is intelligence:
  1. **The correction loop as the accuracy story** (r2 #1) —
     `FoodCorrectionSheet` + `PortionStepper` are LIVE (reality
     discrepancy 3); corrections become stored priors.
  2. **One clarifying question in the vision EF** (r2 #2) — cap at one,
     collaboration not interrogation.
  3. **Meal memory** (r2 #3) — "again" is in the book; elevate to a
     ≤6-tap repeat with yesterday's corrected plate as the portion
     prior. Cheapest accuracy AND retention win; repeat meals carry
     proven outcome evidence (5.9% vs 4.3%).
  4. **Partial + retro legitimacy** (r2 #4) — describe-path equal rank;
     a 1-2 entry day renders as a KEPT day in the book, never sparse-
     shamed (≥2 occasions/day is the clinical adherence bar).
  5. **Day-29 décrescendo** (r2 #6) — median logging life is ~a month;
     after week 3-4 offer lighter modes (photo-only, protein-only days)
     instead of silence; re-entry without guilt.
  6. **GLP-1 action pair stays the glance layer** (r2 #5): kcal quiet,
     protein-vs-floor lead, fiber earns via constipation payoff,
     fractional "ate a third" honored (the reading's fraction control
     exists). Sodium/micros remain doors, never dashboard.
- DB strategy is ALREADY the recommended one (pantry > USDA > OFF live
  at runtime; reality §FOOD) — keep; never import OFF (ODbL).
- Evidence: r2 throughout; r1 #6 (half-portion problem is our home turf).

## 6. MEDICATION (v24 THE REGIMEN) — KEEP · additive gaps only

- The audit's clearest result: v24's architecture (version chains,
  deterministic dose events, converging surfaces, actionable reminders)
  is ahead of the entire category (r1 preamble); the category's
  most-loved feature (PK curve) is pseudo-quantitative and our
  provenance law already refused it. Do not rebuild anything.
- Genuine gaps, all additive (r1 implications 1-9, r5 #4):
  1. **Cycle-day framing** — "day 6 of 7; appetite often returns about
     now; nothing is wrong" on Today/regimen home. The honest
     substitute for the PK curve.
  2. **Food noise as a vital** — a 0-10 log tied to dose timing in the
     pattern engine; the category's best idea (MeAgain proved the aha),
     ours to do honestly.
  3. **Missed-dose label facts at log time** — the late face gains the
     label's own rules (per-product, from the catalog), phrased as
     facts + "your prescriber decides", never advice. The most-Googled
     panic moment; nobody does it.
  4. **The maintenance era** — goal-reached shifts cadence support to
     protein/strength/drift-watch on the SAME chains; regain honesty.
     Nearly unbuilt category-wide; the retention answer (the record
     that survives the prescription outlives the category).
  5. **Underreported-symptom vocabulary** — fatigue, hair shedding,
     menstrual changes, temperature, mood join the side-effect chips
     (3 severities, no sliders — law holds). 400k-post evidence; no
     competitor covers it; pairs with the 41%-suffer-in-silence wedge.
  6. **Switch/travel beats** — new-med era records its why (insurance,
     side effects, cost — never equivalence claims); travel surfaces
     the wall-clock promise ("your tuesday shot stays tuesday").
  7. **HOLD/SLOW titration states** (clinic side, r5 #4) — real-world
     titration is slower than label; the prescribed chain must express
     "hold at 5 mg, reassess in 4 weeks" without deviation framing.
- Work item: v24 shipped ZERO analytics events (reality discrepancy 6)
  — instrument before iterating.

## 11. WORKOUT / SESSIONS — REMOVE the library · REBUILD movement

- Ten questions: the 128-exercise/1,105-voice-clip/Lottie NTC-lite
  answers a problem dedicated fitness apps solve better, at 23%
  historic engagement, with zero wedge (r4 do-not-build #1). The
  founder's instinct is confirmed by evidence.
- What movement becomes (r4 implications 1-5, 8):
  1. **The adaptive walking action** on Today — 60th percentile of her
     own last 9-10 days, floor ~2,500 / ceiling ~8,000, recalcs daily,
     breathes DOWN after hard weeks; rendered as the gap ("2,100 steps
     left"), never the 10,000 slogan.
  2. **The post-meal walk** — contextual offer after the day's biggest
     logged meal; glucose framing (proven), gentle for the medicated.
  3. **The strength floor** for the medication cohort — 2×/wk, 20-30
     min, 4-6 movements, one hard set each, "keep what you built"
     register; guided text first, no video arms race. (25-40% of GLP-1
     loss is lean mass; steps DROP after starting — the slot is open
     and clinical.)
  4. **Movement snacks** as the zero-momentum fallback (2-5 min; VILPA
     honesty).
  5. **Absorb external workouts** — HealthKit-written sessions
     auto-complete the movement action; never ask for what iOS knows.
- Deletion honesty: the engine/content stack (WorkoutGenerator,
  ExerciseBank, voice clips, music, AirPlay scene, Lottie fleet) is the
  era's largest removal; a small exercise-demo SLICE may be salvaged
  for the strength floor. SessionLogRecord sync stays (history is
  hers). Sequence the kill with the strength floor's arrival — never a
  gap where "move" renders nothing.

## 19. CARE PLATFORM + CLINIC DASHBOARD — IMPROVE (the b2b half)

- Reality: the S4/S5 loop WORKS end-to-end on dev and survived v24
  (audit/02); nothing is deployed; the dashboard is list-only.
- The v25 clinician product is r5's evidence-backed shape:
  1. **The attention queue** — exception-based, exactly FOUR signal
     classes (dose-gap ≥14d · weight-velocity outliers · symptoms
     persisting outside the post-escalation window · red flags),
     reviewable <10 min/day; weekly digest for everything else; red
     flags interrupt, never queue.
  2. **The pre-visit one-pager** — the S3 packet grows into the
     flagship artifact (interval weight, dose ledger with gaps,
     symptom timeline, flags raised/resolved; printable).
  3. **Prescribed programs as version chains** with HOLD/SLOW states +
     the 4-week review clock.
  4. **Structured coach knobs only** — titration-plan picker,
     per-patient thresholds, emphasis toggles, vetted templates,
     escalation routing. NEVER free-text prompt editing (no shipping
     precedent; liability; converts the clinic into a model deployer).
  5. **No inbox.** Structured check-ins → digest; urgent-only
     interrupts (message volume = the #1 burnout driver).
  6. Micro-PRO ≤4 taps, titration-windowed, decay-planned.
- The pitch is CHURN (50% discontinue by month 12; under-reporting
  wedge: 41% silent, 11% self-adjust secretly) — never RPM billing
  (manual data never bills; cash-pay has no payer).
- Regulatory posture (r5 tripwires): dashboard built to the §520(o)
  CDS-exemption criteria (HCP-facing, transparent basis); patient side
  stays general-wellness; AI identity disclosed; "— jeni" never signs
  as the clinic; red-flag catalog versioned + remotely updatable.
