# 74 — THE WHOLE DISTANCE

**feat/app-v2 · built 2026-09-03, after 73.** The founder's question:
now that the lens is easy to choose (p73), what should Becoming
actually TELL? Method: three parallel research sweeps written down
BEFORE any hierarchy decision (`74_evidence/00_research_synthesis.md`
— tracker landscape from raw App Store review feeds, GLP-1
communities + clinical titration literature, Apple/third-party chart
detail patterns), then the current page filmed as a customer at every
lens over realistic multi-month seeded histories, then one coherent
decision, built and re-walked. One founder steer arrived mid-pass
("pop ups have a gap at the bottom of screen") and was taken.

## 1. BECOMING'S JOB (the decision)

The research ranks the jobs people actually open a progress page for:
① is the number moving, net of noise (the trend, not the day — Happy
Scale's decade-tenure reviews are this sentence as a product);
② how fast, over the period I chose; ③ for a medicated customer,
what happened at each dose; ④ is the last week making me misread the
longer trend. Everything this pass built serves those four; everything
it removed served none of them.

**One law above all (the Cronometer failure, verbatim in its own
forum): a spoken number must derive from the drawn fold.** Every new
sentence — window delta, rate, era rows — reads the SAME
`WeightWeekReadEngine` fold the chart draws, so the page cannot
disagree with itself. The era ledger's three rows now sum exactly to
the hero's "down 16.9 lb since you started" by construction.

## 2. WHAT THE FILMS CONVICTED (as-found, `01–05`)

- **The lens changed almost nothing that mattered.** Week and month
  rendered the identical weight hero ("4 weeks", same sentence);
  a year lens drew a six-month scribble with no events (`01` vs `02`).
- **No rate of change existed anywhere.**
- **The consistency card** — a hero-scale streak counter ("you've
  shown up 3 days in a row" over a six-month record, `03`) — is the
  exact adherence-grade class the review research shows users
  distrust on sight (Cal AI's health score, Noom's streaks).
- **The weight tile duplicated the hero** pixel for pixel; "read the
  whole week" opened a page that repeated its own headline twice.
- **The dose-era ledger — the category's most-praised GLP-1 read —
  already existed and was buried** under a 400pt tally strip inside
  a below-the-fold tile (`05`); dose seams could never draw at
  week/month (28-day window vs a 57-day-old change).
- **Raw weigh-ins drew in full ink over a hairline trend** — the page
  led with exactly the noise its own words say to ignore (backwards
  per MacroFactor's grammar).
- The weight detail's "the record" row claimed the 4-week window was
  the record while the hero above said "since you started" — a
  summary contradicting its own page.
- "protein reached 130g on **0 of 6** logged days" · "your dose
  landed **0 of 1**" — grades in facts' clothing.

## 3. THE NEW HIERARCHY (why each seat)

**HERO — the progress story** (`06`–`09`): numeral · the LENS'S OWN
sentence · the whole distance beneath, always:
- week: "down about 1.3 lb this week." (the standing band read)
- month/3mo: "down 3.7 lb this month. about 0.9 lb a week."
  (`BecomingStory.windowRead` — new pure engine; the observed rate,
  never a projection)
- year/all: "down 16.9 lb since you started. 38.9 lb to go."
- the trend is now the INK line, raw weigh-ins the quiet context —
  the reassurance job drawn as geometry (`16` is the whole argument
  in one film: calm ink over bouncing gray)
- dose seams draw on the hero when in-window.

**THE DOSE SEAT** (GLP-1 only, directly under the hero, `06`): "YOUR
DOSE · week 9 at this dose · 1 mg · down 7.5 lb · 8 wks" — one
compact card, never a medical dashboard; absent regimen = absent
seat. Opens the medication page.

**INSIGHTS** — only when something happened: the chemistry delta
cards stay (±8% floor); the consistency card is CUT; its slot goes to
the research's #1 reassurance job — `steadyContext`: "this week reads
flat. the month is still down 4.1 lb." — which speaks ONLY when the
week's band is honestly `holdingSteady` AND the month's covered trend
moved (pinned both ways: a falling week gets no reassurance, a flat
month gets no false comfort).

**THE GRID** — better edited: calories + protein tiles (the pair),
chemistry + week-rails as rows (p73 window honesty kept), waiting
rows unchanged. Weight and medication left the grid (the hero and the
seat are those objects); steps joined the rows (a week-locked rail is
a row's worth of information).

## 4. FULL-SCREEN DETAIL (the founder's direction)

The detail pages were rebuilt per metric rather than stretched:

- **WEIGHT** (`08`–`10`): its own range chips (initialized from the
  page lens — temporal continuity; local after that, deliberately:
  exploring inside a page must not yank the page she returns to),
  230pt chart with dose seams, hold-then-drag scrub that speaks
  "aug 12 · 182.4 lb" (date + value), the window ledger with honest
  span labels ("6 months · from 199.1 to 181.2 lb" — "the record"
  label lie is dead), the ERA ROWS in words, and the door to the ink
  scene. The era rows and the hero derive from one fold and cannot
  disagree.
- **MEDICATION** (`13`): dose + weeks-at-dose lead, the era ledger
  SECOND, the tally strip demoted to a small labeled 40pt strip.
  A young era (< 4 weeks — the titration-interval floor from the
  clinical literature) reads "week 3 · early to read", NEVER a rate;
  suppression strips every numeral (both pinned).
- Other metrics keep the editorial template (numeral · chart · read
  · ledger · stance · provenance).

**Interaction defect fixed on the way** (walk-caught, real): the
scrub gesture's `minimumDistance: 0` claimed every touch on a chart,
so a full-screen page whose chart sits mid-viewport could not be
scrolled by dragging on it. The grammar is now Apple Health's own —
touch and HOLD (0.18s) then drag to scrub; an immediate drag stays a
scroll.

**The founder's mid-pass steer** — the expansion sheet stopped a
safe-area short of the screen bottom at every detent, showing a
dimmed strip of the page beneath. The detent math governs the top
edge; the bottom now always reaches the screen (`10` — paper runs
under the floating tab bar).

## 5. THE INK SCENE — THE WHOLE DISTANCE (`11`, `12`)

Becoming's one dark moment, and the pass's name: from the weight
page, a record ≥ 8 weeks old opens **the whole distance** — the
entire trend drawn in paper stroke on ink, dose seams where they
happened, "down 16.9 lb since march." in the big serif, "38.9 lb to
go." beneath. Dark means "the whole story", chosen on purpose —
never an interruption, never decoration. The first cut arrived as a
sliding cover and film caught the seam; it now CROSSFADES per the
§4.8 ink grammar (0.55s in, 0.4s back to paper; Reduce Motion
arrives whole). It is the page she'd screenshot — the
celebration-shaped moment of this pass, reached by her own hand.

## 6. TRUTH GATES (all pinned in `BecomingStoryTests`, 12 tests)

- A window claim requires the trend to actually COVER ≥ ~3/4 of the
  window — a 30-day sentence from 12 days of record is refused.
- An up month is never scolded and carries no rate; a flat month
  says "held about steady" with no rate.
- The flat-week reassurance requires both halves separately honest.
- A dose era younger than 4 weeks never carries a rate ("early to
  read"); numeric suppression strips every era numeral; no regimen,
  no seat.
- "your dose landed 0 of 1" over a still-open slot is silence now
  (`WeeklyBodyReviewTests` +1); protein's "reached 130g on 0 of N"
  states the average and the floor instead.
- No projections, no goal dates, no PK curve, no cross-user
  comparison (refused; see §8).

## 8. REFUSED, WITH REASONS

- **A PK/medication-level curve** — real category demand, but
  modeled-not-measured (v24's standing refusal, now re-validated
  against the provenance law).
- **Cross-user / trial-curve comparison** — against the never-a-grade
  law; a population curve is not her record.
- **Projected goal dates** — the research's own failure mode: a
  projection that moves without visible reason burns trust
  (Cronometer, MacroFactor complaints); observed rate only.
- **Customizable card layout** — the product does the thinking:
  existence-gating (seat only when medicated, seams only when they
  exist, insights only when earned) beats a settings screen.
- **Renaming the hero door per lens** — p73's stable-name law; it is
  "the whole story" at every lens (renamed once, because its
  destination changed from a week summary to the whole weight page).
- **A 7-day weight chart at the week lens** — a week of trend is
  noise drawn large; the chart keeps its 4-week floor and names it
  on its face (the p73 window-honesty precedent).
