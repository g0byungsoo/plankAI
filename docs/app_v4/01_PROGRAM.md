# app v4 — the program (per chapter)

Date: 2026-07-06. The ideal low-effort weight-care program each
cohort should live inside, and the deterministic model that renders
it. Evidence tags cite `docs/app_v4/research/PROGRAM_STRUCTURE.md`,
`JOURNEY_UX.md`, and the v3 verified base (`docs/app_v3/research/`,
`docs/app_v2/SCIENCE.md`).

Design laws carried forward: lowest possible effort; nothing renders
absence; no streaks; no locks; numeric suppression respected;
provenance-only copy (every number traces to a collected field);
no medication instructions; no first-party weight-loss claims.

---

## 0. The shared shape (all chapters)

**The day** (v3, kept): jeni's reading → THE ONE THING → rhythm
rows → the plate story (band, rebuilt) → evening close. At most one
ask; everything else is ambient or optional.

**The week — the program's heartbeat (new):**
- Weeks run Monday→Sunday. Every week has a NAME and one sentence
  of intent in jeni's voice, derived deterministically from
  (chapter, phase, week index, her flags). Monday is a fresh page
  (fresh-start effect [strong]).
- Sunday evening (or first open after): **THE RE-SIGNING** — jeni
  reads the week back from her data (receipts grammar), then at
  most ONE proposal with the reason attached, consented:
  "keep it" / "adjust" / "not this week" (never punished, [strong]
  MacroFactor anatomy + Tate 2006 license). Proposals draw from a
  closed, safe set: protein floor ±5g (within cohort bands), workout
  days ±1 (within tier bounds), week-intent choice for the coming
  week, weigh-cadence softening, or "hold steady" (a proposal of no
  change, stated with its reason — holding is also signal).
- The re-signing writes a signed adaptation record; the journey
  renders it as a moment ("week 3 · your plan eased protein to 95g
  — because you cleared 90g five days straight").

**The arc**: chapters are made of PHASES (below). Phase names
pre-interpret days; position renders quietly ("week 4 of 15" small,
the name large). Early program leads with "N kept"; past the
midpoint the lead flips to "N to go" (Koo & Fishbach [strong]).
Day 1 is endowed with its reason: "day 1 of 105 — your intake built
this plan" (Nunes & Drèze [strong]).

**Scoring**: kept days (lifetime, never resets) + week standing
(the same kept/partial/quiet grammar at week scale). Two built-in
rest days per week are planned progress. No chains.

## 1. losing — the general weight-loss program

Phase skeleton (scales to `plan.totalDays` by fraction, with two
fixed-clock gates; minimum shapes hold down to ~8-week plans):

1. **finding steady** — weeks 1-2 (fixed). Stability and noticing
   before pushing: snap rhythm, first weigh-ins, the reading habit,
   protein introduced as care. Evidence: stability-skills-first cut
   regain in women (Kiernan 2013 [strong]); early self-monitoring
   predicts outcomes [strong]. Week intents: "the arriving week,"
   "finding steady."
2. **the early read** — weeks 3-4 (fixed gate). The engine reads
   her actual response (weigh trend + rhythm) and the re-signing
   proposes plan v2 — pace hold, protein shift, or rhythm change —
   framed as the plan learning HER, never a verdict on her
   (<2% at wk 3-4 → 5.6× failure risk; named "early rescue" in the
   literature [strong]; our register: "the plan just learned you").
3. **the build** — the long middle until ~72% of plan. Themed weeks
   rotate through the DPP-derived lanes in our voice [strong]:
   protein week, food-noise week, environment week (kitchen &
   cues), stress & sleep week, movement-you-keep week, restaurant &
   real-life week, begin-again week (self-compassion; scheduled,
   not triggered). The rep + reader carry the theme; the one thing
   biases toward it.
4. **the bend** — a pre-named plateau phase entered by DATA (EMA
   flat ≥3 weeks while rhythm holds) or by CLOCK (~month 3),
   whichever first. The plateau is adherence decay, not metabolism
   (Hall [strong]) — so the bend is a support phase: novelty blocks
   (new rep formats, new week themes), a steady-week option
   (maintenance practice framed as skill, defensible per Wing/
   Kiernan; NOT a metabolic "refeed" — MATADOR failed free-living
   replication [strong]), and honest physiology copy.
5. **the last stretch** — final ~15%. "N to go" leads; the
   re-signing begins asking the keeping question early: "when we
   get there — hold, or keep going?"
6. **the hold** — final 2 weeks. Stability practice at goal pace
   (Kiernan again): the program proves she can stand still before
   it lets her graduate.
7. **graduation** → keeping chapter (earned moment, sticker tier).

Day defaults (unchanged from v3 where good): snap anchor daily;
protein target from TargetsService; steps 7,500 ambient; workouts
ONLY on scheduled days (sessionsPerWeek), 5-minute floor, never the
center; rep daily (20-60s); breath as the brake + rest-day hero;
weigh Mon/Thu (softened cadences for risk cohorts).

## 2. on-medication — the companion program

Open-ended (no fixed end date; medication timelines are hers, not
ours). Rolling structure:

1. **settling in** — weeks 1-2. Protein floor as the hero number
   (adequacy framing, 1.6 g/kg frame [strong advisory]); the
   under-eating net arms ("did you eat enough today?" — inverted
   posture on low days [white space]); sit-check introduced next to
   plates; movement = keeping strength while she changes.
2. **strength blocks** — rolling 4-week intents thereafter, rotating
   by her data: protein-adequacy block, strength block (muscle
   anxiety is real; "Ozempic muscle" discourse [moderate]), rhythm
   block (meals-as-structure when appetite is quiet), the-quiet
   block (what to do with food noise gone — reward re-fill, identity
   reps [moderate]). The re-signing proposes the next block with the
   reason from her week.
3. **the maybe-after** — always visible, never pushy: one journey
   card naming that a stopping plan exists when she wants it
   ("whenever that chapter comes, we have a plan for it"). The
   consensus is behavioral support through discontinuation [strong];
   nobody serves it at consumer price [product-fact].

Day defaults: protein floor hero; snap ≤3 interactions with
sit-check chip; weigh weekly (Monday — pharmacological loss makes
daily noise anti-signal); appetite self-note optional (NEVER an
asserted injection cycle — refuted [strong]); workouts framed
exclusively as strength-keeping; celebration NEVER for eating less.

## 3. keeping — the post-GLP-1 / graduate program

1. **the first six weeks** — settle phase, contact-INTENSE (inverse
   tapering; regain half-life ~23 weeks means weeks 0-26 are the
   battle [strong]; support consensus 87-100% [strong]). Weekly
   intents: settle weight named ("your settle: 65.2 kg"), the band
   drawn, weigh pattern established (pattern beats frequency
   [moderate]), 3-plate weekly logging floor (maintenance minimum
   effective dose [moderate]), strength anchor.
2. **kept** — open-ended. The band IS the program: steady /
   drifting / reset zones on the EMA (STOP Regain, SNAP-validated
   in women 18-35 [strong]). Zone crossings OPEN NAMED WEEKS, never
   alerts (null-trial law [strong]): drifting → "the steadying
   week" (a real composed week: protein floor back up, plate floor
   up, a named skill); reset → "the reset arc" (multi-week,
   supported, framed as physiology not failure). Kept-weeks scoring
   (weigh pattern + plate floor + movement rhythm). Monthly status
   moment keeps the earned identity alive.
3. **entry always asks**: "hold here, or keep going?" — at
   graduation, at migration, at goal-reach (post-GLP-1 women often
   don't self-identify as maintainers [strong]).

Day defaults: weigh Sunday cadence + daily-capable capture with EMA
narration; plates 3+/week floor (not daily pressure); movement
rhythm over minutes; reading tone shifts to satisfaction + identity
(maintenance motivation is satisfaction, not approach [strong]).

## 4. The deterministic model (engineering contract)

New pure types beside PrescriptionEngineV2 (no engine rewrites):

- `ProgramArc.phase(programDay:totalDays:chapter:) -> Phase` —
  pure math over the skeletons above; every phase carries name,
  register line, and range.
- `WeekIntent.intent(week:phase:chapter:flags:seed:) -> Intent` —
  named week + one-sentence brief + beat biases (which beat the
  one-thing prefers; which rep pillar leads). Deterministic seed =
  dayKey of the week's Monday.
- `WeeklyReview` — assembles the Sunday receipt from WeekState +
  proposes ≤1 adaptation from the closed set + records consent.
  Storage: device-local JSONL (FoodLogPersister pattern), userId
  keyed, additive only. Applied adaptations write the SAME knobs
  the settings sheet already writes (protein delta key, session
  count within tier) so TargetsService/engines pick them up through
  existing reads — no schema change, no server change.
- Copy renders through the existing voice pipeline (DailyBriefEngine
  gains arc context; InsightEngine gains week-story mode) so push,
  tab, and chat never disagree.

Hard fences: pace floors + goal math untouched; safety gate and
numeric suppression respected everywhere; the three Today hooks
(shown-up, session, anchor refresh) re-wired never dropped;
notification ids follow the 4-site protocol; BreakState pauses the
week clock (a held week never grades).
