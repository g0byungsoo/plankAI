# app v4 — THE JOURNEY (the plan-over-time surface)

Date: 2026-07-06. The calendar-strip answer. Evidence:
`research/JOURNEY_UX.md` (grammar verdicts, receipts law, adaptation
anatomy, framing science).

## The decision (approaches weighed)

**A. Restore the strip on Home** — rejected. The founder's Home law
("if Home is busy, it failed") killed a 70pt strip once already, and
a strip alone is the "illusion of completeness" trap: seven cells
cannot hold plates, weigh-ins, week names, or adaptation moments.

**B. A fourth tab ("plan")** — rejected. Three tenses (today / jeni
/ becoming) are the brand's IA; a fourth tab splits "the story" from
"the plan," which are the same object in a program. Nav restructure
risk for no content gain.

**C. Becoming rebuilt as THE JOURNEY** — chosen. "Becoming" is
literally the journey's name. The tab today is four disconnected
modules and the founder's "does not add enough value" verdict; it
has the mandate to become the place where time is visible. One
surface gains: position, past, adaptation, trend, archive.

Today's thread (the discoverability fix): the masthead day pill's
dead-end sheet is replaced by a **week ribbon** on Today — one line
of typography under the masthead: seven standing dots (solid past /
today distinct / dotted future) + "week two · finding steady".
Tapping it (or the pill) opens THE JOURNEY. ~24pt of quiet type, not
calendar furniture; Home stays five things.

## The surface (top to bottom)

1. **Masthead** — "becoming" + the arc line ("finding steady · week
   2 of 15 · day 12"). Early: "12 kept"; past midpoint: "44 to go"
   (Koo & Fishbach). A thin phase ribbon under the masthead: named
   phases as segments, a today tick — the program's shape in one
   hairline object.
2. **THE LINE** (kept, repaired) — the EMA trend canvas + band
   (keeping chapter) with ONE story: the headline sentence and the
   delta badge must read the same window (the v3 dual-window
   contradiction dies). Raw number de-heroed to a tap-in detail.
3. **THIS WEEK** — the open chapter: week name + intent sentence,
   seven day-dots with today live, the week's receipts so far
   (plates n · weigh n · moved n · reps n — only rows with data).
4. **THE WEEKS** — the ledger. Reverse-chron week chapters, each a
   receipt card: week name, standing dots, jeni's week line
   (InsightEngine week-story), micro-artifacts (plate count,
   protein-days, weigh delta in EMA terms, steps-vs-usual), and —
   when one exists — the SIGNED ADAPTATION stamp ("your plan eased
   protein to 95g — because five days cleared 90"). Weeks with no
   data compress to a quiet seam ("a few days passed") — absence
   never renders (Cordeiro/Eikey law). Break weeks render "on a
   break · your place was kept."
5. **Tap a week** → the week page: the seven days as receipt rows
   (standing + what landed), the week's plates as a photo strip,
   its weigh-ins on a mini line, the re-signing record. Tap a day →
   **the day receipt**: everything true about that day — standing,
   beats kept, plates (photos, protein), weigh-in, steps-vs-typical,
   and jeni's reading from that morning (the reading is
   reconstructable: the brief engine is deterministic over that
   day's stored inputs). Read-only memory, not homework; backfill
   only through existing flows (weight sheet, snap).
6. **THE FUTURE** — one card, dotted ink: next week's name + shape
   ("a lighter week: 3 moves, the protein floor holds") — shape,
   never a locked list, withdrawn to week-shape granularity beyond
   ~3 days (Flo's uncertainty rule).
7. **Doors** — her plates (full food history), her weigh-ins, her
   file (chat dossier). The journey is also the archive index.

## Motion + register

Gentler Streak's band-and-line warmth in JeniFit's serif editorial:
hairlines over cards, ink solid/dotted as the tense grammar, no
month grid anywhere. Entrance: the phase ribbon draws first, then
week cards settle in a soft cascade (existing Motion tokens; reduce
-motion = opacity only). The re-signing moment is its own full-screen
received page in the note register (cream, cascade, consent doors)
— the app's second cinematic gesture, used once a week.

## What dies

HerDaysSheet + the day-pill dead door; the v1 food-journal interior
as a destination (FoodLogTimelineView's jobs fold into the journey's
plates layer + day receipts); ProgramDayPeekSheet's padlock-era
framing (future = shape card); Becoming's "OPEN her food journal"
row, "THE WEEK, HELD" one-liner, and method card in their current
forms — their jobs are absorbed by the ledger.
