## LANE 1 — what evidence says about structuring the weeks

### 1. The DPP arc — the canonical curriculum [strong]
DPP Research Group, Diabetes Care 2002;25(12):2165-71 (PMC1282458).
16-session core curriculum, completed **within the first 24 weeks**,
30-60 min sessions, individual case-manager model. Goals: ≥7% weight
loss + 150 min/wk activity. The famous outcome (58% diabetes incidence
reduction; Knowler et al., NEJM 2002) made this THE template.

Core session order (Lifestyle Balance):
1. Welcome (program + self-monitoring) · 2. Be a Fat Detective ·
3. Three Ways to Eat Less Fat · 4. Healthy Eating · 5. Move Those
Muscles · 6. Being Active: A Way of Life · 7. Tip the Calorie Balance ·
8. Take Charge of What's Around You · 9. Problem Solving · 10. Four
Keys to Healthy Eating Out · 11. Talk Back to Negative Thoughts ·
12. The Slippery Slope of Lifestyle Change · 13. Jump Start Your
Activity Plan · 14. Make Social Cues Work for You · 15. You Can Manage
Stress · 16. Ways to Stay Motivated.

Shape of the arc: **mechanics first (self-monitoring, food, movement,
energy balance: sessions 1-7) → environment + psychology second half
(stimulus control, problem solving, cognition, relapse, social, stress,
motivation: 8-16)**. After core: face-to-face at least every 2 months +
phone between, group refresher courses (4-8 wk), 3-4 motivational
campaigns/yr, ~$100/participant/yr "toolbox" for individualized
adherence fixes. Common translations (CDC National DPP lineage) compress
to 16 weekly core sessions (~4-6 months) then monthly maintenance
[strong].

### 2. Dose + duration effects
- USPSTF 2018 evidence review (Curry et al., JAMA 2018;320:1163):
  effective intensive behavioral programs mostly had **≥12 sessions in
  year 1**; ~⅓ used an explicit core phase (3-12 mo) + maintenance
  phase (9-12 mo). [strong]
- Extended contact beyond month 6 improves maintenance: Perri 1998
  review (Clin Psychol Sci Pract) [moderate]; Middleton, Patidar &
  Perri 2012 meta-analysis (Int J Obes / PMID 22212682): extended care
  g=0.385 ≈ **3.2 kg better maintained** over mean 17.6 months
  post-intervention vs control. Perri's own caveat (1988): maintenance
  programs may postpone rather than prevent regain. [strong]
- Contact frequency: weekly contact during active loss is the norm in
  every effective trial arm (DPP weekly-ish core; standard academic
  structure = 24 weekly sessions → biweekly → monthly). No RCT isolates
  weekly-vs-biweekly cleanly [weak for the isolated comparison; strong
  that effective programs front-load frequency and taper].
- Tate, Jackvony & Wing 2006 (Arch Intern Med 166:1620-5, N=192):
  weekly **computer-automated tailored feedback matched human e-mail
  counseling at 3 months** (−5.3 vs −6.1 kg, control −2.8); by 6 months
  human counseling pulled ahead (−7.2 vs −4.9 vs −2.6). [strong] →
  automated weekly review is legitimate early; the relationship layer
  matters more as novelty fades.
- Svetkey et al. 2008 WLM trial (JAMA 299:1139-48): after initial loss,
  brief **monthly personal contact** beat interactive-technology-only at
  30 months (−1.2 kg difference); IT-only's early advantage over
  self-directed faded by 30 mo. [strong] → post-program cadence =
  monthly, personal-feeling.

### 3. The 6-month reality (plateau is a scheduled event)
- Franz et al. 2007 systematic review (J Am Diet Assoc 107:1755-67,
  80 studies ≥1-yr follow-up): diet ± meds → **5-9% loss in first
  6 months, plateau at ~6 months**, 3-6% still held at 48 months, no
  group back to baseline. [strong]
- Mechanism: Thomas/Hall adherence model (Am J Clin Nutr 2014,
  PMC4135489): the ~6-mo plateau timing is explained by **intermittent
  loss of adherence, not metabolic adaptation** (adaptation changes
  final weight, not plateau timing); Freedhoff & Hall (Lancet 2016):
  "exponential decay of diet adherence." [strong]
- → Program design: the plateau is an ENGAGEMENT event on a fairly
  predictable clock (month 3-6 of active loss; earlier for small
  deficits). Treat it as a named phase with expectation-setting BEFORE
  the nadir, and respond with adherence-reengagement + self-compassion,
  never "fix your metabolism." [strong inference]

### 4. Does sequencing matter? (what to front-load)
- **Early response predicts everything.** Unick et al. (Look AHEAD
  analyses; Curr Diab Rep 2017, PMC5789799): failing to lose ≥2% by
  week 4 → **5.6× more likely** to miss 10% at 1 year; 1-2-month loss
  predicts weight up to 8 years out. Authors explicitly propose
  stepped "early rescue" for 4-week non-responders. [strong]
- **Self-monitoring consistency ↔ weight loss** — Burke, Wang & Sevick
  2011 systematic review (J Am Diet Assoc 111:92-102): more complete
  diaries → more loss; within-person, high-monitoring weeks are
  losing weeks. Confirmed in mHealth-era trials (Burke 2025, Obesity).
  [strong] → self-monitoring is week-1 curriculum, not a later skill.
- **Maintenance-first sequencing works (in women).** Kiernan et al.
  2013 (J Consult Clin Psychol 81:336-46, N=267 women): 8 weeks of
  "stability skills" BEFORE any weight loss (practice holding weight,
  daily weighing to learn fluctuation, savoring small amounts of
  favorite foods, low-effort satisfaction) → same 6-mo loss, but
  **significantly less regain over 12-mo follow-up with no staff
  contact**. The only true topic-ORDER RCT found, and it's in
  JeniFit's demographic. [strong, single RCT]
- Sequential vs simultaneous diet+activity: mixed. CALM trial (Ann
  Behav Med 2013, N=200): only the simultaneous arm met all behavior
  recommendations at 12 mo; other trials show no difference. [moderate,
  mixed] → theme weeks may FOREGROUND one skill, but both behaviors
  stay present every week.
- Honest gap: beyond Kiernan, there is no RCT of curriculum topic
  order. DPP's mechanics→psychology order is precedent, not proof.
  [weak for exact order; strong for "self-monitoring + early-win
  mechanics first"]

### 5. Flexible restraint (rule design inside the structure)
Westenhoefer et al. 1999 (Int J Eat Disord 26:53-64) + follow-on work:
**flexible control** (graded choices, portion awareness, compensate
later, no forbidden foods) associates with lower disinhibition, lower
BMI, less binge eating, higher probability of successful loss;
**rigid control** (all-or-nothing rules, fasting, guilt) associates
with the opposite. Mostly correlational [strong association, moderate
causality]; one RCT in resistance-trained adults (Conlin 2021, JISSN)
found flexible ≈ rigid for loss with better ad-lib outcomes [moderate].
→ Program rules should be written as bend-don't-break ("about half,"
"fits," compensation grammar) — which is already JeniFit's register.

### 6. Check-in cadence
- Weekly program-level review has direct RCT support via Tate 2006
  (automated tailored weekly feedback ≈ human at 3 mo) [strong] and is
  the universal spine of DPP/academic programs [strong].
- Self-weighing cadence (daily capture, smoothed weekly narration,
  young-women affect caution): already settled in
  docs/app_v3/research/GLP1_RESEARCH_2026_07_05.md — daily weighing +
  zone feedback evidence (Wing 2006 STOP Regain; SNAP 2016) with the
  Pacanowski 2023 caution. Not re-argued here.
- Post-program: monthly brief personal contact (WLM) [strong].

### 7. Diet breaks / refeeds — the MATADOR honesty check
- MATADOR (Byrne et al., Int J Obes 2018;42:129-38): 2-wk-on/2-wk-off
  energy restriction in obese MEN, food-provided/supervised → greater
  fat loss + less regain vs continuous. [moderate — one tightly
  controlled trial, males, not free-living]
- Free-living replication FAILED: Headland, Clifton & Keogh 2019 (Int
  J Obes 43:2028-36, N=332, 85% female completers, 12 months):
  week-on/week-off intermittent restriction = **no advantage** (−5.1 kg
  vs −6.6 kg continuous, ns). [strong null] BREAK trial protocol
  (PLOS One 2023) still pending results.
- → For a behavioral app, scheduled "maintenance weeks" are an
  ADHERENCE/psychology tool (planned flexibility, rest, Kiernan-style
  stability practice) — defensible framing — NOT a metabolic hack.
  Never promise "reset your metabolism." [strong inference]

### 8. GLP-1-era structure
- **Behavioral intensity adds little to drug-phase weight magnitude:**
  STEP 3 (Wadden et al., JAMA 2021;325:1403-13; 30-session IBT +
  8-wk meal replacements + semaglutide → 16.0% at wk 68) vs STEP 1
  (18 brief clinic visits + semaglutide → 14.9%). IBT tripled placebo
  loss but added ~1 point on-drug. [strong] → an on-GLP-1 companion
  should NOT be a calorie bootcamp; its jobs are side-effect
  navigation, protein/muscle preservation, movement, persistence, and
  building the skills that matter AFTER.
- Published companion-program structure is thin. Industry pattern
  [product-fact, first-party]:
  - Omada Enhanced GLP-1 Care Track (2024 press + outcomes releases):
    GLP-1 content layered ON TOP of the core lifestyle curriculum —
    titration education, side-effect mitigation, nutrition/protein,
    exercise-specialist muscle-preservation support; company-reported
    persistence + weight outcomes (first-party, not peer-reviewed).
  - WeightWatchers GLP-1 Companion Program: Points de-emphasized;
    daily currency becomes **protein + fruit/veg + water targets**;
    injection/side-effect logging; tailored workshops (first-party).
  - Noom Med: psychology curriculum + medication management
    (first-party).
  No RCT of a titration-phase-aligned curriculum was found. [weak
  evidence base; clear product convergence]
- **Post-GLP-1 regain clock:** systematic review + nonlinear
  meta-regression (eClinicalMedicine, online March 2026; 6 RCTs,
  n=3,236 in the model): regain follows an exponential-recovery curve,
  **half-life ~23 weeks; ~60% of lost weight regained at 1 year**,
  extrapolated plateau ~75% of lost weight. [strong] → the first ~6
  months off-drug is the entire battle; a post-GLP-1 chapter should be
  densest in weeks 0-26 after stopping.
- **Structured taper exists as practice, not proof:** Embla (Danish
  digital clinic; ECO 2024 presentation, Medscape/Healthline coverage;
  N=2,246): at target weight, taper to zero over ~9 weeks (step-down
  every 2-4 wk) + nurse/coach/psychologist lifestyle support → weight
  stable through 26 wk post-discontinuation (mean additional −2.1%
  during taper). Observational, self-selected completers, first-party
  clinic data, no control arm. [moderate at best; label as practice
  pattern] JeniFit cannot manage dosing (compliance floor) but can
  mirror the SHAPE: a named, supported, multi-week off-ramp phase.

## LANE 2 — what makes a program FEEL custom

### 1. Tailoring works, modestly [strong]
- Noar, Benac & Harris 2007 meta-analysis (Psychol Bull 133:673-93,
  print tailoring): tailored beats generic/targeted; effect small.
- Lustria et al. 2013 meta-analysis (J Health Commun 18:1039-69,
  web-delivered tailoring): significant short- AND long-term behavior
  effects; mechanisms = feedback, personalization, adaptation.
→ Personalization is a real but small main effect. Its bigger role in
a paid app is retention/trust, which the health literature measures
poorly. [moderate inference]

### 2. PERCEIVED personalization is the active ingredient [moderate]
Li 2016 (Comput Hum Behav 54:25-33): actual personalization only
works through **perceived** personalization; perception mediates
engagement/attachment effects. (Marketing context, not health.)
→ Adaptation the user never notices buys nothing. Surface it: name the
inputs, show the change.

### 3. Explanation, rationale, and the transparency curve
- Kizilcec 2016 (CHI): when an algorithmic outcome VIOLATED
  expectations, an explanation preserved trust; but excessive
  transparency (too much detail) eroded it again — inverted-U.
  [moderate, ed-tech context] → in a weight program, expectation
  violation is scheduled (the plateau, §3 above): that is exactly when
  "because you X → we Y" must fire. One clear reason beats a data dump.
- Self-determination theory: providing a meaningful rationale supports
  internalization of uninteresting-but-important behaviors (Deci,
  Eghrari, Patrick & Leone 1994, J Pers). [moderate] → every
  prescription carries its one-line why.
- Buell & Norton 2011 (Manage Sci 57:1564-79), the "labor illusion":
  showing the work during a wait INCREASES perceived value vs instant
  identical results (effort → reciprocity). [strong, services context]
  JeniFit's receipt-tape loader (live keys only) is already the honest
  version of this. Keep it honest: show REAL work only.
- JITAI framework (Nahum-Shani et al. 2018, Ann Behav Med): adaptation
  should key off observed state at decision points — the conceptual
  backbone for "your data changed → the plan changed." [strong
  framework, not an effect estimate]

### 4. Product practice — how the best-in-class do "feels custom"
All [product-fact], first-party where noted:
- **Runna**: pace targets auto-adjust from logged performance +
  post-workout feedback; visible "your paces have been updated"
  moments; new plan blocks build on the previous one instead of
  resetting; weather-adjusted sessions (2026). Periodized named phases
  (base/build/taper convention).
- **MacroFactor**: fixed **weekly check-in ritual** where expenditure +
  targets recalc from the user's own weigh-ins + food logs; a whole
  help article ("How should I interpret changes to my energy
  expenditure?") exists because the explanation surface IS the product;
  program styles (coached / collaborative / manual) let users pick
  agency level; coaching modules audit data hygiene so the algorithm
  stays credible.
- **Noom**: ~16-week core psychology curriculum, 5-10-min daily
  lessons, concepts introduced gradually and re-encountered (spaced);
  "4Cs" engagement model (first-party blog).
- **WeightWatchers**: the weekly weigh-in/workshop is the ritual spine;
  GLP-1 companion swaps the currency itself (Points → protein/produce/
  water) — cohort membership changes the GRAMMAR, not just copy.
- **Fabulous**: journeys with unlockable phases (three "mountains"),
  one keystone habit stacked into rituals; progress = narrative
  advancement, not just metrics ("2 weeks to feel better" claim is
  first-party Duke-affiliated marketing — treat as unverified).
- **Embla/Omada**: phase-LABELED medical arcs (titration → target →
  taper → maintenance) — patients always know which chapter they're in.

Shared grammar of "feels custom": (1) a **named plan/phase** the user
can point to; (2) **visible re-plan moments** triggered by the user's
own new data; (3) a **"because you X → we Y" sentence** at each change;
(4) a **fixed weekly ritual** that anchors when adaptation happens;
(5) plans that **carry history forward** instead of resetting.

### 5. The line between honest adaptation and personalization theater
- Li 2016 implies theater "works" short-term (perception suffices).
  But Kizilcec shows trust collapses on expectation violation unless a
  REAL explanation exists — and in weight care the violation (plateau,
  regain scare) is guaranteed by month 3-6 (§3). Theater is a time
  bomb timed to detonate exactly when retention is decided. [moderate
  inference, strong for JeniFit given the plateau clock]
- Operational line (matches the house data-provenance rule): every
  adaptive statement must trace to a collected field; every "we
  changed X" must name the datum that caused it; never imply
  computation that didn't happen; show real work during real waits
  (labor illusion, honest variant); if nothing changed this week, SAY
  nothing changed — a stable plan honestly narrated reads as
  confidence, not neglect. [design rule, grounded in above]

## implications: the JeniFit program arc

Program length is per-user (10-20 wk from goal math) → define phases as
**percentages of the derived arc + fixed-clock events**, not hardcoded
weeks. The plateau clock and the early-response clock are ABSOLUTE
(week 3-4 check, month ~3-6 bend), while curriculum phases scale.

### Universal skeleton (all cohorts)
1. **THE SETUP — weeks 1-2 (fixed).** Stability + self-monitoring
   first: the rep, snap rhythm, weigh rhythm, one-thing grammar. Teach
   fluctuation literacy (Kiernan) before ambition. [strong: Burke 2011,
   Kiernan 2013, DPP sessions 1-4]
2. **EARLY-RESPONSE GATE — week 3-4 (fixed).** <~2% trend → named
   rescue re-plan: simplify to ONE THING, recalibrate targets, raise
   contact warmth; framed as "the plan bends to you," never failure.
   ≥2% → visible plan-v2 confirmation. [strong: Unick; this gate is
   also the first big "because you X → we Y" moment]
3. **THE BUILD — ~15-60% of arc.** One themed skill per week,
   DPP-derived, post-Ozempic vocabulary: environment/food noise at
   home · eating out/fits · thought patterns (talk back) · slippery
   slope/lapse resets · social cues · stress + sleep · motivation
   maintenance. Both food + movement stay present weekly (CALM).
   Weekly automated review ritual = "her week" reading. [strong:
   DPP order as precedent, Tate 2006 for automated weekly review;
   moderate: exact topic order unproven]
4. **THE BEND — begins ~month 3 or ~60% of arc, whichever first.**
   Pre-named plateau phase, foreshadowed in THE BUILD ("the bend is
   coming; it's in the plan"). Response kit: adherence re-engagement
   (monitoring restart, not harder rules), one flexible-restraint
   "steady week" (maintenance-calorie practice framed as Kiernan
   stability training, NOT a metabolic reset), expectation copy from
   Franz/Hall. [strong: plateau timing + adherence mechanism; strong
   null: no metabolic diet-break claims]
5. **THE KEEP — final ~15% of arc.** Maintenance-specific curriculum:
   settle weight + zone education (BandModel already shipped), regain
   response rehearsal, identity consolidation; handoff to rhythm mode
   with monthly-cadence check-ins after graduation (WLM), presence
   ledger continues. [strong: Perri/Middleton extended care, Wing
   zones, Svetkey monthly contact]

### Cohort skews
- **Losing (general WL):** full skeleton as above.
- **On-medication:** NOT a calorie bootcamp (STEP 3). Re-theme phases
  to the medication reality: SETUP adds side-effect navigation +
  protein-adequacy + muscle preservation as the primary currency
  (WW/Omada pattern; protein nets already shipped); BUILD emphasizes
  eating skills learned "while the noise is quiet" — explicitly seeded
  as skills-for-after from mid-arc (Kiernan logic applied forward);
  BEND reframes as titration/persistence support; KEEP doubles as
  pre-off-ramp. No drug names on app surfaces (compliance floor).
  [strong for STEP 3 + Kiernan transfer; product-fact for structure]
- **Keeping (post-GLP-1):** maintenance-first is the WHOLE arc, not
  the tail. Densest support weeks 0-26 off-drug (regain half-life
  ~23 wk; 60% regained by 1 yr if unsupported). SETUP = zones +
  daily-capture/weekly-narration + protein/strength; BUILD = appetite-
  return skills as food noise returns; BEND maps to the steepest part
  of the regain curve (~months 2-4 off-drug) with drift-gate actions
  (app_v3 ledger: act at ~1-2 kg EMA drift); KEEP = permanent rhythm.
  Mirror the Embla SHAPE (named, supported, multi-week off-ramp)
  without touching dosing. [strong: regain trajectory; moderate:
  Embla pattern]

### Personalization grammar (cross-cutting)
- Every plan change ships with one "because [your datum] → [change]"
  line — one reason, not a dashboard (Kizilcec inverted-U; SDT
  rationale; Li perception).
- Re-plans are NAMED and visible (plan v2 at the week-3 gate; the bend
  plan; the keep plan) — Runna/MacroFactor pattern; history carries
  forward, plans never visibly reset.
- The weekly review is the fixed ritual where adaptation is allowed to
  happen (MacroFactor pattern; Tate 2006 evidence) — adaptation
  outside the ritual only for safety/drift gates (JITAI).
- Provenance rule is absolute: no adaptive claim without a collected
  field behind it; a genuinely unchanged week is narrated as stability,
  not disguised as adaptation. Theater detonates at the bend.
