# Low-effort retention mechanics research (2026-07-05, verified)

Method: 6 search tracks (~110 findings, ~60 primary sources) →
3-vote adversarial verification of 32 load-bearing claims (0
refuted, 6 corrected). First-party/marketing numbers labeled.

Calibration: paid D7 10% is a real fire — health&fitness D7 median
~10% for ALL installs; paying users should retain top-decile.
Annual billing hides engagement death ~11 months then surfaces as
non-renewal + refunds (category refund rate 4.71%, 2nd highest —
RevenueCat 2025). Noom first-party: top engagement quartile stays
subscribed 6.2 mo vs 2.8 bottom; 2x trial renewal. **~55% of
3-day-trial cancellations happen Day 0** (RevenueCat platform-wide).

## Track A — evidence

- Micro-reps: EMI within-person g=0.57 (g=0.45 self-guided; Versluis
  JMIR 2016); SMS-only programs −2.28 kg across 12 RCTs (Skinner,
  Obes Rev 2020); "effective engagement" consensus (Yardley 2016).
  Noom N=11,252: outcome variance sits in logging (R²=.16) and
  weigh-ins (.17), NOT articles (~.01-.05) (Carey JMIR mHealth 2021,
  Noom-authored). MOOC median completion 12.6%; real-world mental-
  health apps: median daily open 4%, 15-day retention 3.9% (Baumel
  JMIR 2019). → the rep replaces the lesson; lessons become a
  browsable library, never a track she falls "behind" on.
- If-then rehearsal: d=0.65 goal attainment (Gollwitzer); eating
  d≈0.51/0.29 (Adriaanse 2011), stricter meta d≈0.33/0.18 (Carrero
  2019) — real, not magic. Volitional help sheet RCT ELIMINATED the
  stress→snacking link for a week (O'Connor/Armitage, Ann Behav Med
  2015). Add-good beats suppress-bad. → "tonight plan" rep: Jeni
  proposes one if-then from her real pattern; one tap to keep.
- Urge surfing digital: Eat Right Now 40.2% craving-eating reduction
  in N=104 women — SINGLE-ARM, never present as RCT (Mason, J Behav
  Med 2018). → 60s "ride the wave" tool from Today + notification.
- JITAI: adaptive beats static (g=0.89 Wang&Miller 2020; sober 2025
  BMJ MH meta g=0.15 between-group); <6-week interventions most
  durable (g=0.71); HeartSteps prompts decay ~3%/day → ~4-wk
  half-life per mechanic. Lapse prediction ~80% from BRIEF
  self-report EMA (Goldstein/Forman, Health Informatics J 2020);
  longer EMA = worse adherence, zero gain. OnTrack overall null vs
  WW alone but doubled loss in one subgroup — lapse pings AMPLIFY a
  structured plan. → front-load adaptivity weeks 0-6; rotate prompt
  mechanics ~3-4 wk; triggers from signals we hold (no plate by her
  usual hour + evening); 1-2 taps max.
- Notifications: fixed daily 8pm anchor = 3.5x next-hour opens, NO
  disengagement cost over 30d (Bell, JMIR mHealth 2023); receptivity
  peaks 10am-6pm + weekend midday (+11.8% at 12:30pm Sat/Sun,
  Bidargaddi 2018); send-time ML didn't beat plain daily (Morrison
  2017); prompts announcing NEW content win (RR 1.27, Alkhaldi
  2016); iOS opt-in ~44% (Airship; their retention lifts are
  correlational — do not build on). Calm/Amplitude: reminder-setters
  retained ~3x; asked RIGHT AFTER first session, 40% set it. → ONE
  anchor at her hour, asked at end of FIRST completed ritual; body
  always carries news about HER.
- Weekly artifact: automated tailored WEEKLY feedback doubled loss
  and matched human coaching at 3 mo (Tate, Arch Intern Med 2006);
  reflection dialogues on passive data → 48% kept using unpaid
  (Kocielnik IMWUT 2018); daily micro-feedback still beats weekly
  for adherence — the receipt complements, not replaces. →
  Sunday receipt + MacroFactor-style consent-based re-prescription.
- Weight framing: raw daily graphs harmed women 18-22 (affect
  lability d=0.84, Pacanowski 2023/24); mid-life daily weighing safe
  and effective (Steinberg RCTs); day-to-day variability predicts
  WORSE outcomes (Benson/Lowe IJO 2020, N=24k); normal intra-week
  fluctuation ~0.35% body weight, peaks MONDAY (Turicchi PLOS ONE
  2020). NO published RCT of smoothed-vs-raw display (in-house A/B
  opportunity). ≥30-day weigh gaps coincided with +0.6-1.4 kg
  (10k-user cohort). → trend/steadiness language leads; pre-explain
  Monday bumps; weekly cadence default for youngest; weigh-GAP is a
  warm re-engagement signal.
- Consistency metric: ≥10% losers logged ~20.8 d/mo vs 11.2
  (Harvey 2019, 90.8% female); simplified tracking preserved most
  outcome IF weekly personalized feedback remained (Nezami 2022).
  → score presence days against a ~2/3 rhythm; praise, never grade.
- Passive → insight: tracker feedback alone +1,800 steps, −1 kg
  (Ferguson, Lancet Digit Health 2022 umbrella); plain-sentence
  patterns from passive data changed behavior (Health Mashups TOCHI
  2013); PH-LLM near-expert insights from wearables (Nat Med 2025)
  but NO RCT that LLM narrative beats templates for retention. →
  every passive datum earns ONE authored sentence; no raw dashboards.
- Streaks: broken-streak DISPLAY alone suppresses re-engagement
  (Silverman & Barasch JCR 2023); characterological lapse
  attribution predicts worse loss (r=−.36, AVE); dichotomous
  thinking predicts REGAIN (Byrne 2004); 73% of ED patients said
  trackers fed the disorder (Levinson 2017). Reserves beat hard
  goals (rebound 55% vs 37%; Sharif & Shu); missing one day doesn't
  derail habits (Lally); fresh-start framing works (Dai/Milkman);
  self-compassion reduces post-lapse overeating (doughnut study;
  Turk & Waller meta r=−.34/−.44). Duolingo's streak is secretly a
  reserve system (2 equipped freezes = +0.38% DAU; cites Sharif&Shu).
  → KEPT DAYS: lifetime count that never resets + weekly rhythm
  with 2 built-in rest days + automatic repair; presence-based,
  never food-perfection.

## Track B — teardowns (labeled first-party where so)

- Cal AI: acquisition-led, thin day-2+ loop; REMOVED from App Store
  Apr 2026 (IAP bypass + deceptive paywall). → the day-2-to-30
  vacuum is the opportunity; honest billing is a category trust
  wedge now.
- MacroFactor: weekly user-scheduled CHECK-IN (algorithm reviews →
  explains → recommends → asks CONSENT); adherence-neutral; logs
  visibly teach the model. → the most stealable mechanic; Sunday
  Jeni check-in = receipt + consent-tap re-prescription; "this week
  taught your plan: protein −5g."
- Noom: curriculum fatigue by wk 4; Sept 2025 rebuild = microhabits
  + system insights (+20% D30, first-party). → validates beats-over-
  lessons; instrument weekly engagement quartile in PostHog.
- Gentler Streak (ADA 2024): rest as first-class prescription; life
  status "sick/injured/on a break" pauses expectations. → ship the
  break state; emit rest beats deliberately ("soft on purpose").
- Oura/Whoop: passive in → ONE authored morning verdict out; Oura
  ~$500M 2024, women in early 20s = named growth market; Advisor
  valued for interpretation + MEMORY (60%/87% first-party). Failure:
  score-anxiety (orthosomnia 3-14%). → Jeni speaks first; numbers a
  tap deeper; never a score hero.
- Apple: ring closure is binary satisfaction but zero rest
  tolerance ("real addictive behavior"); Trends/Highlights =
  system-authored observations; Workout Buddy cheer-without-insight
  got disabled by users; monolithic AI coach (Mulberry) wound down
  Feb 2026. → closure FEEL without dead streak; authored lines must
  carry a datum; ship small authored surfaces, not one chat-brain.
- Duolingo: CURR focus; widget (half of widget users ≥6-mo streaks);
  milestone animation +1.7% D7 new users; Friend Streak +22%
  (first-party); their Gardenscapes clone = zero effect (mechanics
  don't transfer without context). Toxic here: leagues (compulsion),
  guilt copy (AVE cascade), loss-aversion on the tracked behavior.
  → widget (stretch), reserves, ONE milestone bloom in week 1,
  notification discipline. Never leagues/guilt.
- How We Feel / Rise / Opal: one interaction per open; Rise = ONE
  passively-computed number, 14/28 daily open (first-party); Opal
  keeps one non-metric delight mechanic. → one-word check-in ≤30s;
  an open is never worthless; keep one pure-delight beat.
- Headspace/Calm/Balance: winning sessions 1-3 min; Calm reminder
  experiment (above). → shortest complete unit is the default dose.
- Finch (~75% women 25-35): the pet is never disappointed; pausable
  streaks; time-boxed gift returns. → Jeni notices, never
  disappointed; "something is ready" return hooks that are GIFTS
  (receipt drops Sunday, trend note "finished computing").
- Lumen/Simple/Flo: morning input REWRITES the day (consequence
  makes ritual); AI-coach-as-spine at $100M scale (Simple,
  first-party); Flo dailies keyed to HER timeline (+30% opens from
  Stories test) + every experiment scored on retention. → morning
  weigh/word visibly re-tunes today; score every ship on retention.

## Ranked 12 for the D7 fire
1. Jeni speaks first (morning authored verdict from passive data).
2. Sunday week receipt + consent-based check-in re-prescription.
3. Kept days + reserves, never reset; automatic repair.
4. Day-0 post-purchase ritual (paywall-grade design; 55% D0 stat).
5. One anchor notification, asked at peak motivation (post-ritual).
6. Sub-60s daily rep replaces the lesson.
7. Every log visibly teaches the plan.
8. Rest days + "on a break" as first-class states.
9. Lapse-moment support ping (weeks 0-6 only, 1-2 taps).
10. Weight steadiness framing + physiology-normalizing copy.
11. Home-screen widget (ritual state + kept days) — stretch.
12. One-tap food-noise/appetite word check-in (EMA seed).

## 5 anti-patterns (never)
1. Reset-to-zero streaks / perfect-day chains on eating.
2. Guilt/loss-aversion notification copy.
3. Leagues/leaderboards/social comparison on food or weight.
4. Raw daily score as hero (calorie red, body score, raw weight).
5. Fixing retention with MORE content or MORE notifications.
