## Q1 — Post-GLP-1 maintenance mechanics (early drift detection)

### STOP Regain three-zone architecture [strong]
Wing RR, Tate DF, Gorin AA, Raynor HA, Fava JL, NEJM 2006;355(15):
1563-71 (N=314, mean prior loss 19.3 kg). Daily self-weighing →
traffic-light zones with escalating corrective action:
- steady: within ~1.4 kg (~3 lb) of settle weight → reinforcement
- watch: +1.4–2.2 kg (~3–5 lb) → deploy a problem-solving strategy
- reset: ≥2.3 kg (5 lb) → restart active-loss effort (mailed toolbox)
18-month results: 72.4% of controls regained ≥2.3 kg vs 45.7%
face-to-face / 54.8% internet arms (p<0.01). Daily self-weighing
carried the effect (p<0.001). Note: color labels formalized later in
SNAP as Go!/Caution!/Stop!; green-zone width is ~3 lb not ~2 lb.

### SNAP trial — zones validated in JeniFit's demographic [strong]
Wing RR et al., JAMA Intern Med 2016;176(6):755-62. N=599, ages
18-35, 78% women. Daily self-weighing + red/yellow/green feedback
(web/SMS → immediate automated zone feedback + monthly zone-matched
tips). ~3 yr: −2.37 kg (Large Changes) vs +0.26 kg control, p=0.001;
obesity incidence roughly halved. Caveat: gain-PREVENTION population,
not post-loss maintenance.

### Recovery from regain is rare — intervene early [strong]
Phelan/Hill/Lang/Dibello/Wing, AJCN 2003;78(6):1079-84 (N=2,400
maintainers): of relapsers (≥5% regain), only 4.7% returned to
baseline; 12.9% re-lost half the gain by year 2. Hayes 2022 Obesity
30(12):2404-13 (N=2,457, 94.9% female): successful re-engagers acted
at 2.3 kg regain vs 4.5 kg for unsuccessful; sustained ~16 wk vs 10.
[moderate]. Average procrastination point ≈ >4 kg regained.
→ Intervene at ~1–2 kg EMA drift, frame as care; reset = multi-week
supported arc, not a day.

### HARD CAUTION: drift alerts without action systems are NULL [strong]
- NULevel (Sniehotta, PLoS Med 2019, N=288): daily smart-scale +
  regain-intensifying SMS → −0.07 kg difference, p=0.9.
- LIMIT (Daley/Jolly/Madigan, NIHR PHR 2019;7(7), N=583): encourage
  daily weighing + ≤1 kg target → +0.53 kg (null).
- NoHoW (JMIR 2026;28:e69634, N=1,627): motivation/emotion-regulation
  toolkits added NOTHING over weighing+Fitbit control.
→ The zone crossing must OPEN a concrete skills-based action path +
relationship-grade contact. The action system IS the product.

### Weighing PATTERN beats frequency [moderate]
Brockmann/Eastman/Ross, Obesity 2020;28(7):1215-18 (N=74): total
frequency not associated with 9-mo change (p=.141); weeks weighing
6-7 days predicted less regain. → Score "consistent weeks," treat a
broken weighing pattern as the earliest drift signal; never scold.

### Daily weighing risk is population-dependent [strong]
Pacanowski 2023 (RCT, 69 university women 18-22): daily weighing →
greater negative affective lability (d=0.84). Benn 2016 meta (N=10,775):
overall no harm, but negative associations stronger in younger
samples. In successful maintainers it is protective (Wing 2007).
→ Capture daily, DISPLAY smoothed EMA; "gentler cadence" mode for
body-image-sensitive users. Measure daily, narrate weekly.

### Appetite-return timeline: pharmacology inference only
No RCT measured appetite-return timing. Anchors: semaglutide t½ ~7d,
tirzepatide ~5.4d → substantially cleared over ~4-5 weeks [strong].
Regain meta-regression (Budini, eClinicalMedicine 2026-03-04, 6 RCTs,
N=3,236): regain half-life ~23 wk; ~60% of lost weight regained by
1 yr; plateaus ~75.3% [strong]. Mechanism = return of hyperphagia
(appetite hormones back online; Reiss, Biomolecules 2025) [strong].
→ Lean in during the ~4-5 wk post-stop window. NEVER state "appetite
returns on day X."

### Consensus: behavioral support during discontinuation [strong]
Modified Delphi (Obesity Pillars 2025): if GBT interrupted, "close
post-therapy follow-up and repeated lifestyle intervention" (87%),
"intensive behavioural therapy" (100%), protein-rich patterns (67%).
Regain framing: "predictable disease biology"; personal-failure
framing is "subtly stigmatizing" (Cureus 2026 [moderate], use for
language not numbers). NO RCT validates a specific taper protocol —
open gap = the white space. Position: "evidence-informed behavioral
support for the stopping window," never a proven substitute.

## Q2 — On-GLP-1 daily lived experience

- Food noise: "switch turned off overnight" (Pierret, Acta Diabetol
  2024); constant food thoughts 62%→16% (Kvist INFORM n=550
  [moderate], Novo-sponsored); Food Noise Questionnaire validated
  (Diktas, Obesity 2025) [strong]. → "food noise" is peer-reviewed
  vocabulary; the app is "what you do with the quiet."
- The QUIET reveALS a void: grief/flatness/anhedonia when food
  reward leaves (APA Monitor 2025; Cook, Cureus 2026) [moderate].
  → CBT/breath/chat lane: "the quiet revealed something"; re-fill
  reward with non-food wins.
- UNDER-eating is the nutritional risk: only 43% hit ≥1.2 g/kg
  protein, 10% ≥1.6; 98.6% below vitamin-D/potassium DRI (Johnson,
  Front Nutr 2025, N=69; GNC-affiliated authors, awareness) [strong].
  → protein as adequacy FLOOR ("enough to feel strong"); a quiet
  "did you eat enough today?" under-target safety net = white space.
- Fear of eating signal: FAERS ROR 3.35 (Chen, Front Endocrinol
  2024); NEJM Perspective 2026-04-25; no standard ED pre-screen
  [strong for the signal]. → never celebrate eating LESS; celebrate
  eating WELL; hunger is information.
- Social eating + identity: meals-as-social-glue disrupted; "every
  picture shows a different person"; body-image lag persists
  [strong/moderate]. → scripts for social meals; identity thread
  honors continuity ("she got you here"), no before/after shock.
- Muscle anxiety: "Ozempic muscle" / "skinny-fat" questions rising
  (Medscape 2026) [moderate-strong]. → movement = "keep your
  strength while you change."
- INJECTION-DAY APPETITE RHYTHM: REFUTED as established science.
  Blog/forum lore only; steady-state plasma is flat on ~7d half-life.
  → NEVER assert a weekly cycle. Offer neutral self-logged appetite
  input; reflect HER pattern back ("some people notice...").

  side-effect support + hydration/fiber + muscle-preservation
  movement. Noom made its GLP-1 Companion FREE 2026-06-24. → cannot
  differentiate on the checklist; win on voice, cohort fit, loop.
- Noom: pivoted to GLP-1 commerce (compounding acquisition 2026-04);
  Lilly terminated its LillyDirect integration 2025-07-25. Trust
  wounds: auto-billing traps + generic-AI-coaching complaints.
- WW: Ch.11 filed 2025-05-06, emerged PUBLIC 2025-06-24 (~$1.15B
  debt cut); pivot to menopause+HRT + Novo partnership; Dec 2025 app
  = fullest GLP-1 feature set (no points for GLP-1 users, 3 daily
  targets, protein ~1 g/kg STARTING weight, strength ≥2×/wk, dose/
  site/side-effect log). NO off-medication product; midlife brand.
  → 22-35 is open; "3 daily targets" validates a beats-like model;
  1 g/kg starting weight = citable formula.
- Omada: IPO 2025-06 (OMDA). Enterprise before/during/after GLP-1
  lifecycle — clearest off-ramp IA; 7,500-step goal (same anchor).
  Complaint: coverage conditioned on engagement = coercion backlash.
  → mirror before/during/after; NEVER gate on compliance.
- MyNetDiary GLP-1 Companion (2026-05-05): protein hero with
  explicit 80-120 g/day band; digestion-symptom tracker EMBEDDED in
  the food diary ("how did this sit?" next to meals); "non-bossy"
  4.8★. → symptom-next-to-meal chip is the smartest side-effect UX;
  keep snap loop ≤3 interactions.
- Prescriber tier (Found/Ro/Hims): drug access commoditized
  (LillyDirect/NovoCare $199-449/mo direct vials); zero wraparound
  support. Novo terminated Hims 2025-06-23. → stay prescriber-
  agnostic; never touch supply.
- Off-ramp owners are clinic-priced only: Calibrate (taper data,
  B2B pivot), Embla (peer-reviewed: ~36% of standard cumulative dose
  with slow taper + coaching still lost median 14.8% by wk 64).
  → consumer post-GLP-1 mode = clearest white space. "Taper is a
  phase with a protocol, not an ending."
- RD marketplaces (Fay $50M @ $500M val; Berry Street $50M; both
  2025-02): $0-with-insurance dietitians, episodic. → the visit-to-

- NPR investigation (2026-06-14): mandatory weigh-in/logging as

## Q4 — Maintenance-phase product design

- Different motivational system: initiation = approach; maintenance
  = satisfaction + relapse-avoidance (Rothman 2004; Kwasnicka 2016
  HPR). West 2011 (N=338 women): satisfaction/identity-focused
  maintenance == skills training at 18 mo [strong].
  → copy pivots "becoming her" → "keeping what you built"; surface
  achieved benefits as satisfaction fuel.
- Relationship-grade contact is the active ingredient: Perri TOURS
  2008 + JAMA Netw Open 2020 (phone == face-to-face); meaningful-
  counselor calls attenuate regain, unknown-interviewer calls don't.
  MAINTAIN (Voils, Ann Intern Med 2017, N=222): satisfaction +
  relapse-planning + monitoring + social support halved regain
  (0.75 vs 2.36 kg) [strong]. → scheduled recurring Jeni check-in
  with memory of her data; script = MAINTAIN's four blocks.
- Logging minimum effective dose in maintenance ≈ 3 days/wk
  (Arroyo, Obesity 2024, N=74; best 5-6) [moderate]. → "3 plates a
  week" floor; escalate only on drift.
- JITAI for maintenance = unclaimed: 2025 scoping review (Koh, JMIR,
  35 studies/19 JITAIs) found ZERO targeting maintenance/relapse-
  prevention [strong]. OnTrack mechanic (EMA check-in → lapse-risk →
  matched micro-intervention; Forman 2019) [moderate]. → rule-based
  "risk pulse" on zone crossings / weighing-pattern breaks =
  first-in-category.
- Boredom is a named failure mode: Maintenance-Tailored Therapy
  (Levy, Prev Med 2010) novelty blocks trended (p=0.078)
  [weak-moderate]. → rotate themed blocks as engagement, not as a
  claimed treatment.
- WW maintenance architecture: graduation after a 6-wk settling
  phase; lifetime status KEPT via monthly weigh-in within ±0.9 kg
  (2 lb); ~80% held ≥5% at 1 yr but only ~16-26% stayed below goal
  weight (Lowe, BJN 2008) [moderate]. → graduation moment + earned
  status + monthly status weigh-in in a ±2-3 lb band; success =
  "kept ≥5% off," never "back to goal."
- Post-GLP-1 users often do NOT self-identify as maintainers (LIMIT
  observation) [strong]. → ask explicitly: "hold here, or keep
  going?"; intensify contact in the first 3-6 months after stopping
  (inverse of classic tapering).

## Persistence data (why the off-ramp is mainstream)
~65% of non-diabetes GLP-1 users discontinue within 1 year, ~85% by
2 years (Truveta / JAMA Netw Open 2025; Prime Therapeutics 2021
cohort — date-stamp any persistence stat; newer cohorts persist
somewhat longer as shortages resolved) [strong].

## Ranked takeaways (1-10)
1. Post-GLP-1 off-ramp = the franchise bet (white space, mainstream
   scale).
2. Ship the three-zone drift ribbon on the EMA canvas as maintenance
   mode's spine.
3. The action system, not the alert, is the product (null-trial
   graveyard).
4. Injection-day rhythm: never assert; user-discovered only.
5. Under-eating is the on-GLP-1 risk; protein floor + "enough"
   safety net = white space.
6. Regain is physiology, not failure — say it out loud (consensus
   backs the register).
7. Intervene at 1-2 kg drift, before the 4 kg procrastination point;
   reset = supported multi-week arc.
8. Table stakes are commoditized and free; win on voice + cohort +
   loop quality + honest terms.
9. Maintenance is a distinct mode: protection framing, graduation,
   earned status, ±2-3 lb band, 3-day logging floor, "hold or keep
   going?" asked explicitly, contact intensified for first 3-6 mo.
10. Rule-based maintenance JITAI ("risk pulse" → matched micro-move)
    = first-in-category, explainable, optional-by-design.

## Do-not-use list
- Any precise "appetite returns on day X."
- The 0.6 g/kg meal-skipping figure (needs primary verification).
- The 18-30-month body-image-lag figure (secondary source).
- Company outcome stats as evidence (Noom/Omada/WW/Calibrate/Embla
  numbers are marketing data; Embla's is peer-reviewed but self-run).
- Any assertion that a zone system is validated for POST-DRUG regain
  specifically (evidence is behavioral-loss maintenance; drug-
  cessation regain is faster — thresholds may trip sooner; honesty
  point for clinical review).
