# app v3 — the minimal correction (premium = fewer, better)

Date: 2026-07-06. Founder verdict on the premium pass: Home got
BUSIER — the note-as-block adds weight; premium must not mean more
typography, more cards, more hierarchy. This pass corrects with
taste: less surface, more interaction, one memorable moment.

## The brutal Home audit (counting objects)

Before this pass Home stacked SEVEN visual objects: masthead · date
eyebrow · THE NOTE (card: dateline + 2 serif sentences + mechanism +
reply + signature) · the strip (7 cells + marker) · the cocoa card ·
rhythm rows · the band. Two cards compete as heroes; the date
renders twice (masthead eyebrow + note dateline); the user reads
~60 words before the ask. Verdict: a designed prototype's density,
not a premium product's calm.

## The correction

**Home keeps FOUR quiet things and ONE dark anchor:**
1. Masthead (day pill · chapter note · two marks) — the pill becomes
   TAPPABLE → "her days" sheet (see below).
2. JENI'S LINE — one serif sentence, no card, no chrome, with a
   quiet "from jeni ↗" affordance. Tap → THE NOTE as a FULL-SCREEN
   moment: cream field, the letter cascading in line by line (the
   her75 LineCascadeText + soft haptic per line — the app's one
   cinematic reveal), dateline + both sentences + mechanism +
   signature, REPLY → chat, "keep it" → close. The reading is
   RECEIVED in focus, not consumed as a block while scanning.
3. THE ONE THING (cocoa) — now the screen's only card, period.
4. Rhythm rows + the band — unchanged bones, the quiet floor.

**The strip leaves Home.** Day-to-day time travel is one intentional
tap: the masthead day pill opens HER DAYS — a calm sheet holding the
full strip (same component, same past-review/future-peek taps) with
room to breathe. Home loses 70pt of calendar chrome; time gets a
dedicated surface instead of ambient furniture.

## Feature interiors (this pass)

- **Past days become memories**: the day review sheet gains the
  day's plates line ("2 plates · 62g protein") from the device
  journal — a receipt, not a standing verdict alone.
- **Steps become passive insight**: the sheet leads with the count
  in serif, a 7-day consistency dot-band (no chart), and ONE
  authored line from her real week ("your legs covered the gap on
  4 of 7 days"); the connect state stays an invitation.
- **Breathwork speaks weight-care moments**: occasion doorways say
  the real reasons (a craving wave / before eating feelings / the
  day went sideways / begin again) and the receipt hands the moment
  back to her day.
- **The rep's chosen door earns its moment**: fill + drawn check +
  a two-stage haptic; the unchosen doors exhale.
- **Workouts stay demoted** (scheduled days only, 5-minute floor,
  long-press "i moved elsewhere" honored) — no further ceremony.

## What this pass will NOT do
No schema/EF/payment/gating changes; no new tabs; no reskins of the
already-excellent interiors (snap carousel, breath session core,
trend canvas, reader); no decoration anywhere.
