Date: 2026-07-06. Founder direction: restore Home's iconography as a

## The JeniFit ritual marks (JKMarks.swift)

Six small product marks, drawn as thin single-weight vector strokes
(1.3pt, round caps, 24-grid Canvas paths) — the register of a good
stationery set, tintable and crisp at any size. One idea each:

- **plate** — a circle with a morsel resting inside (food / snap)
- **path** — three footfall dots rising along a diagonal (steps)
- **line** — the trend easing down to its small dot (weight)
- **breath** — two nested crescents (the exhale)
- **door** — a doorway with its swing hint (the method / the rep —
  the rep's own grammar: what's the move?)
- **hop** — a soft arch over its landing (move)

jeni's mark stays the text heart ♥ — never drawn, never replaced.
Usage floors: rows and eyebrows only, one mark per row, never in
prose, never repeated decoratively. Home's rhythm rows carry them at
18pt cocoa-secondary; the week receipt at 16pt. Home's object count
and one-viewport fit are unchanged — identity returned at zero
added density.

## Becoming — the field report

Was: trend story → week dot-strip → Sunday block → up to two insight
CARDS → journey card → wins block (six stacked shapes, three of them
saying week-facts in different grammars).

Is: ONE authored voice, then receipts, then data, then the journey:
1. **THE FIELD NOTE** — the trend story's line + its mechanism (or
   the top pattern's) as a single quiet caption + one ask-jeni. The
   insight-card stack is gone; its truths feed the note and the
   receipt.
2. **THE WEEK, HELD** — mark-led receipt rows, chapter-aware and
   provenance-gated: up to two HELD (protein landed N of 7 / her
   legs covered the gap on N days / the line, checked — zero
   verdicts), at most one DRIFTED (the band's steadying/reset week
   for keeping; protein ran quiet — gently, more), at most one
   HELPED (resets this week / reps kept). Quiet weeks earn the
   presence row ("you showed up · the week is young"), never blanks
   and never guilt. The journal door rides beneath.
3. **THE LINE** — the trend canvas + band, demoted below the words
   (interpretation first, data one scroll deeper), all its states
   kept (single-weight acknowledgment, empty invite, scrub,
   window cycling).
4. **THE METHOD JOURNEY** (kept) and the Sunday receipt artifact
   (kept, Sunday-only).

Data provenance: the new "what helped" rows read the week's actual
completed checks (ProgramDayCheckRecord, trailing-7 by itemKey) —
no fabricated pattern ever renders.
