# app v9 — 04 DESIGN 100× (the design constitution, L7)

**Status: LAW (founder, 2026-08-03, added at P0 approval).** The
architecture and the product direction are no longer the
bottleneck. **Design quality is the bottleneck.** We are competing
for an Apple Design Award — not by copying Apple, but by
extraordinary craftsmanship.

## The bar

Do not settle for "clean," "modern," or "premium." Iterate until it
feels truly exceptional: every screen intentional, every animation
physically believable, every transition delightful, every
interaction communicating craftsmanship. The goal is not visual
novelty — it is extraordinary craftsmanship.

## The laws

- **Remove instead of adding. Simplify instead of decorating.**
  Reduce cognitive load; increase emotional impact and visual
  confidence.
- **Increase whitespace, typography quality, photography. Reduce
  UI chrome.** Every screen belongs to the same editorial system.
- **Never generic:** no AI-looking layouts, no generic gradients,
  no generic cards, no common SaaS aesthetics.
- **Custom interactions when they materially improve the
  experience** — and only then. Redesign only when it measurably
  improves clarity, beauty, emotion, or usability; never to look
  different.
- **The unforgettable-interaction principle:** when choosing
  between adding a new feature and making one existing interaction
  unforgettable, always choose the unforgettable interaction.

## The verification duty

Continuously verify on the simulator: exercise every flow, inspect
every animation/transition/spacing/typography/hierarchy decision,
record interactions, analyze frames when necessary. Keep refining
until the product feels like something an Apple designer would
proudly ship.

## Operational rider (how this binds the v9 phases)

- Every phase's shipped record (`05_BUILD.md`) must include a
  **design evidence block**: what was recorded, what was
  frame-reviewed, what was removed (not just added).
- Words are design material: permission strings, consent sheets,
  empty states, and change language get the same craft as motion
  (and still pass founder voice review, D10).
- P1/P2 carry the heaviest 100× duty (the scan + the timeline are
  the signature); P7 remains the dedicated whole-app elevation
  sweep. P0's surface is small but real: the permission story and
  the it-just-works passive-weight moment.
- House laws this composes with, unchanged: the voice law, verb
  law, anti-shame floors, locked tokens, JKBorderBeam placement
  law, scatter milestone rule, a11y floors as tested law.
