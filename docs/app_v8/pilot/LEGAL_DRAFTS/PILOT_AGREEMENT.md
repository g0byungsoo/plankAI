# Jeni Care pilot agreement — WORKING DRAFT (counsel required)

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE, NOT EXECUTED.** A skeleton for counsel.
> Bracketed items and every § marked † need counsel. A BAA is executed
> as an exhibit BEFORE any protected health information flows.

**Parties:** Jeni Health ("Provider") and [Clinic legal name] ("Clinic").
**Effective:** [date]. **Term:** 12 weeks from the effective date,
unless extended or terminated per §11.

## 1. Purpose & scope
A no-fee evaluation pilot of Jeni Care for between-visit adherence and
observation review, visit preparation, and care-plan communication for
Clinic's own patients. Scope is bounded: one clinic, up to [5] authorized
users, up to [20] invited patients, the approved workflow only.

## 2. What Jeni Care is and is not
Jeni Care records the care plan Clinic communicates and organizes
patient-recorded information. It is **not** an EHR, e-prescribing,
pharmacy, monitoring, diagnostic, or clinical-decision service, and it
does not replace clinical judgment. Clinic retains all clinical
responsibility and decision-making. †

## 3. No real-time monitoring; no emergencies
Jeni Care does not monitor patients in real time and is not an
emergency channel. Clinic and its patients use existing clinical and
emergency channels for urgent concerns.

## 4. Milestones & conversion
The pilot progresses through: setup complete → first patient connected
→ first visit-prep review → first assignment → mid-pilot review (wk 6)
→ end review (wk 12). Continuation to a paid agreement is discussed at
the end review against the measures in METRICS.md; nothing here
obligates either party to continue. †

## 5. Fees
None during the pilot. Non-cash consideration is limited to the
feedback and reference rights in §12. †

## 6. BAA (exhibit A) †
A Business Associate Agreement is executed as Exhibit A **before any
protected health information is created, received, maintained, or
transmitted** under this pilot. Until Exhibit A is executed, only
fictional test data may be used.

## 7. Data ownership; return & deletion †
Clinic and its patients own their data. Patient-recorded data belongs
to the patient. On termination, Provider returns Clinic-accessible data
on request and follows the BAA's return/destruction terms; medically
relevant history is retained per RETENTION.md unless the BAA/counsel
directs otherwise.

## 8. Patient consent & enrollment
Patients connect only by their own action, via a code Clinic hands
them, and choose what to share (scoped, revocable). The in-app consent
language is PATIENT_NOTICE.md and is reviewed against the BAA. †

## 9. Staging gates
Fictional data only until the BAA and the security gates in VENDORS.md
are satisfied. Provider operates the pilot in an environment isolated
from its consumer production systems.

## 10. Security & breach notice †
Provider maintains the security posture in SECURITY_STATEMENT.md and
notifies Clinic of any security incident affecting Clinic's data
without unreasonable delay and within [__] days of discovery (no later
than the 45 CFR 164.410 sixty-day floor). Incident handling follows the
runbook.

## 11. Term & termination
Either party may terminate for convenience on [45] days' notice, or
immediately for cause (breach, security incident, or use outside
scope). On termination, clinic access ends and §7 governs data. †

## 12. Feedback, reference & publicity †
Clinic grants Provider the right to use pilot **feedback** to improve
the product. Any use of Clinic's name, logo, quotes, or outcomes in
marketing requires Clinic's separate **written** approval — no
clinic logos, testimonials, or outcome claims are published without it.

## 13. Amendment & correction support †
Provider supports Clinic's ability to amend patient information in the
designated record set (45 CFR 164.526), including copies Provider holds,
and forwards patient-initiated correction requests to Clinic.
