## What Jeni is

Jeni is a consumer weight-care app. It helps you track and understand
your own weight-management effort. It:

- tracks weigh-ins and shows your trend and goal pace
- logs food by photo, label, barcode, or words, and estimates calories
  and macros
- lets you record a weight medication, its dose and schedule, and mark
  each dose taken or skipped
- lets you record how you felt, including side effects
- reads signals you allow from Apple Health (steps, sleep, movement,
  weight, and others) to compose your day
- offers optional private body-progress scans, processed on your phone
- includes jeni, an in-app coach whose replies are generated by an AI
  language model
- offers a paid subscription

**Jeni does not provide medical care.** It does not prescribe,
dispense, adjust, or supply any medication, and it does not create a
clinician-patient relationship between you and us. Recording a
medication in Jeni is bookkeeping about a prescription someone else
gave you. It is not a prescription, and it is not medical direction.

## The in-app coach and AI-generated content

Replies from jeni, nutrition estimates from a food photo, and the

AI, including third-party models (currently OpenAI) reached through
our backend.

- **AI output can be wrong.** Calorie and macro estimates from a photo

  incomplete.
- **AI output is not medical advice** and must not be used to decide
  whether to take, skip, change, or stop any medication.
- Do not rely on Jeni to detect allergens or to tell you whether a
  food is safe for you.
- You are responsible for reviewing and correcting what you log.

## Health and safety

**Jeni is a consumer wellness app, not a medical device, not medical
advice, and not a substitute for a qualified healthcare
professional.**

- **Jeni is not for emergencies.** If you think you are having a
  medical emergency, call your local emergency number or go to an
  emergency department. Do not use the app, and do not message the
  coach, to seek emergency help.
- **Talk to your clinician** before starting a weight-management
  effort or changing your eating or activity, especially if you are
  pregnant or breastfeeding, have or had an eating disorder, have a
  chronic condition, or take prescription medication, including
  GLP-1 receptor agonists.
- **Medication decisions belong to you and your prescriber.** Jeni
  never tells you to change a dose. If your prescriber's instructions
  and anything in the app disagree, follow your prescriber.
- **Targets are general estimates.** Calorie targets, protein floors,
  BMI, weight trends, and goal-pace projections are computed from
  published equations and from what you enter. They are general
  wellness information, not clinical recommendations.
- **Health readings are not clinical-grade.** Values from Apple Health
  or a paired device are shown as reported. We do not validate them
  against medical instruments.
- **No outcome is promised.** We do not guarantee weight loss,
  maintenance, or any health result.
- Stop and seek advice if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, or
  unusual discomfort.
