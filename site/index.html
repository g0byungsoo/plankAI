the problem

The visit is 15 minutes. The month is dark.

Your real calendar is the titration calendar — dose steps every few weeks, side effects triaged in between. But what actually happened since the last visit arrives as memory, a scramble of portal messages, or nothing at all.

So the first minutes of every follow-up go to reconstruction: did she take it, is it sitting badly, is the weight moving, what does she want to ask. Jeni Care hands you that reconstruction before she walks in — assembled from her own records, not another inbox to staff.

how it works

One loop, five steps, no new inbox.

Each step has a clear owner. The clinic authors care; the patient lives it; Jeni organizes it.

Invite a patient

You hand your patient a one-time code. No email matching, no directory lookup, no silent account changes.

clinic · staff or clinician

She chooses what to share

Before anything is shared, she sees who is asking and picks what to grant — her visit-prep record, her daily entries, the ability to assign care — and how far back you can see.

patient · in the Jeni app

Read the record

Open her last four weeks, organized: adherence, weight trend, how meals sat, the protein and movement she logged, and the questions she noted for the visit. Every line is labeled by where it came from.

clinic · clinician

Assign the plan

Record the protocol and the weekly medication plan — name, dose in milligrams, the day it anchors. It becomes her daily care in the Jeni app, clearly marked as from your clinic.

clinic · clinician

She reconciles it

Her app shows the assigned plan beside her own record and asks her to confirm. If something looks wrong, she sends a correction — which lands back here for your decision, and never changes the plan on its own.

the patient experience

The plan arrives as care, not a notification.

Jeni is a warm daily companion she already uses. When your clinic assigns a plan, it doesn't add a portal or a second app — it becomes the medication her day is built around, marked plainly as from her care team.

She confirms it in a single calm moment. Her own earlier records stay in her chart; future check-ins follow your plan. If a name or dose looks wrong, she raises a correction — quietly, never as an alarm — and it waits for your decision.

The medication surface is deliberately clinical: no streaks, no rewards, no celebration. The record itself is the only acknowledgment.

consent · provenance · trust

The patient is in control, and the record proves it.

consent

Scoped and revocable

She grants three things separately — the record, the daily entries, the ability to assign care — chooses a four-week or from-today window, and can turn any of it off at any time.

provenance

Every line is sourced

Patient-entered facts, system summaries, and care-team assignments are labeled distinctly. Self-reported never masquerades as verified.

access ≠ treatment

Revoking stops access, not care

If she disconnects, your clinic loses new access — but her records, the plan you assigned, and the audit trail all remain. Treatment questions go through your usual channels.

audit

Every open is recorded

An append-only trail logs each disclosure — who opened what, and when — visible to your clinic and to the patient about her own record.

not monitored

Reviewed at visits, not in real time

Jeni Care is not an emergency or monitoring service. Records are read when you open them. Urgent concerns go to your clinic or emergency services.

access control

Server-enforced, RPC-only

Clinician access to patient data runs only through audited server functions under row-level security — never a direct query, never a service key in the browser.

the boundary

What Jeni Care does not do.

The wedge is narrow on purpose. Jeni Care records the care plan you communicate; the prescription lives in your EHR and pharmacy.

  • Not an EHR
  • Not e-prescribing or pharmacy transmission
  • No dosing or titration recommendations
  • No prior authorization or claims
  • No insurance or billing
  • No real-time or emergency monitoring
  • No diagnosis or lab interpretation
  • No drug-interaction checking
  • No replacement for clinical judgment
  • No autonomous care

security & privacy

Built for scoped access — and honest about where we are.

Jeni Care is built with patient-granted, scoped consent; auditable clinical actions; row-level isolation; and server-enforced, audited access to patient data. No sensitive health values ever reach analytics or error reporting.

We do not claim to be “HIPAA compliant” — there is no such certification. Before any clinic uses Jeni Care with real patient data, we complete a business associate agreement, a security-rule posture review, and a breach-response process together with your clinic. Until then, every environment you'll see runs on fictional test data.

To review our security and privacy posture for your clinic, start a pilot conversation — we'll share current documentation through an appropriate security review.
